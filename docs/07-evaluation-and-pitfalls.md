# 07 Evaluation, baseline critiques and bias / 评测、基线批判与偏倚

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document collects what the sources say about how perturbation-prediction models are evaluated and where those evaluations fail: baseline critiques, metric pitfalls, community challenges, foundation-model evaluations and ancestry bias. It ends with an evaluation checklist for the genotype-by-drug question.

> 本文档汇集来源关于扰动预测模型如何评测、这些评测在哪里失效的说法：基线批评、指标陷阱、社区挑战赛、基础模型评测和祖源偏倚。它以针对基因型×药物问题的评测清单结尾。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

Deep perturbation predictors have not reliably beaten simple baselines. On CRISPR data, five foundation models, GEARS and CPA did not outperform additive, mean or linear baselines, none beat "no change" at predicting genetic interactions (5,035 of 124,000 candidates at a 5% false discovery rate), and a linear model with perturbation-pretrained embeddings was best; the study covers genetic perturbations only [EV01]. A covariate-only decoder fits RMSE yet is no better than random on a rank metric, and Latent Additive baselines are competitive [EV02]. Across 13 models and 25 datasets, raw-profile Pearson exceeds 0.95, delta and DE metrics discriminate better, and no method transfers across cell types consistently [EV20]; global Pearson saturates at 0.98 to 0.99 for four foundation models while delta metrics separate them [EV12].

> 深度扰动预测器并没有稳定地击败简单基线。在 CRISPR 数据上，五个基础模型、GEARS 和 CPA 都没有优于加性、均值或线性基线，在预测遗传相互作用上没有一个优于“无变化”（124,000 个候选中有 5,035 个，FDR 为 5%），而使用扰动预训练嵌入的线性模型最好；该研究只涉及遗传扰动 [EV01]。只含协变量的解码器在 RMSE 上拟合得很好，但在排名指标上并不比随机好，而 Latent Additive 基线有竞争力 [EV02]。在 13 个模型和 25 个数据集上，原始谱 Pearson 超过 0.95，delta 和差异表达指标的区分力更强，没有任何方法能稳定地跨细胞类型迁移 [EV20]；四个基础模型的全局 Pearson 饱和在 0.98 至 0.99，而 delta 指标能把它们区分开 [EV12]。

Metric critiques have converged on the reference point. Across ten genetic perturbation datasets, perturbed-mean baselines matched or beat GEARS, scGPT and CPA on control-referenced Pearson delta, and re-referencing to the perturbed-cell centroid lowers scores substantially [EV29]; control-referenced deltas reward collapsed predictions when the control is biased, and a DEG-weighted MSE with a technical-duplicate ceiling is proposed [EV30]; metrics are shaped by scale, sparsity and dimensionality [EV31]. Rankings can flip with the metric: a ridge model on fingerprints won under an inverse-variance proxy but ranked fifth of six under an active-set weighted MSE [EV38]. The authors' repository implements the centroid reference and a centroid-accuracy metric; it lists eight datasets where the paper counts ten because one is split into three conditions, and it carries no licence file [EV36].

> 指标批评已经收敛到参照点上。在十个遗传扰动数据集上，扰动后均值基线在以对照为参照的 Pearson delta 上与 GEARS、scGPT 和 CPA 相当或更好，而把参照重设为扰动后细胞质心会使分数大幅下降 [EV29]；当对照有偏时，以对照为参照的 delta 会奖励坍缩的预测，因此有人提出带技术重复上限的 DEG 加权 MSE [EV30]；指标受规模、稀疏性和维度影响 [EV31]。排名会随指标翻转：基于指纹的岭回归模型在逆方差代理指标下获胜，但在活性集加权 MSE 下六个模型中只排第五 [EV38]。作者的仓库实现了质心参照和质心准确度指标；它列出 8 个数据集，而论文计为 10 个，因为其中一个被拆成三种条件，并且它没有许可文件 [EV36]。

Mechanism fidelity and downstream utility are separate yardsticks. In a benchmark of 181 datasets and 423 response cases, expression-similarity metrics aligned only weakly with a Mechanism Fidelity Score [EV33]; simple baselines match perturbation models at reconstructing post-treatment profiles, yet drug-response predictors trained on model-generated profiles outperform those trained on baseline profiles (abstract level) [EV34]. Chemical-to-genetic retrieval succeeded at 0.563 against 0.498 for permuted labels, and seven foundation-model embeddings did not consistently beat gene deltas in K562 (self-reported) [EV37]. TF-activity readouts separated perturbed from control cells only modestly (AUROC 0.597 for decoupleR with CollecTRI) [EV39]. ReconEval tests whether predicted latent shifts decode into correct expression [MD66].

> 机制保真度和下游效用是不同的衡量尺度。在涵盖 181 个数据集和 423 个响应案例的基准中，表达相似度指标与机制保真度分数只有微弱的一致 [EV33]；简单基线在重构处理后谱上与扰动模型相当，但用模型生成的谱训练的药物响应预测器优于用基线谱训练的预测器（摘要层面）[EV34]。化学到遗传的检索成功率为 0.563，而置换标签为 0.498，七个基础模型嵌入在 K562 中没有稳定地优于基因 delta（自报）[EV37]。TF 活性读出只能适度地区分被扰动细胞与对照细胞（decoupleR 加 CollecTRI 的 AUROC 为 0.597）[EV39]。ReconEval 检验预测的潜在偏移能否解码为正确的表达 [MD66]。

Cross-context generalization depends on shared mechanisms and is often overestimated. Cross-context performance often falls to baseline level, and even on synthetic data no model transferred across differing mechanisms (self-reported) [EV32]; standard setups inflate performance, embedding-based splits lower it, and naive dataset merging often degrades results [EV35]. A third-party preregistered study splits the response into a conserved core (30.1% of the energy, 80.8% reproducible) and a context-specific part that kept improving into hundreds of anchors (20 anchors recovered 9% to 13% of the 743-anchor gain; self-reported), so a genotype term likely needs many measurements [EV11].

> 跨背景泛化取决于共享的机制，并且常被高估。跨背景的表现常常降到基线水平，即使在合成数据上，也没有模型能在机制不同的背景间迁移（自报）[EV32]；标准设置会抬高性能，基于嵌入的划分会降低性能，而简单合并数据集常常使结果变差 [EV35]。一项第三方预注册研究把响应分为保守的核心（占能量的 30.1%，80.8% 可重复）和背景特异的部分，后者在数百个锚点之内仍在持续改善（20 个锚点只恢复了 743 个锚点增益的 9% 至 13%；自报），所以基因型项很可能需要大量测量 [EV11]。

Community challenges test the same axis. The Arc 2025 challenge (300 CRISPRi perturbations in H1 hESCs) found that models did not consistently beat naive baselines, that almost all were worse than baseline on MAE, and that winners combined deep learning with statistical features and protein embeddings [EV05]. The 2026 challenge asks for zero-shot knockdown prediction in six unseen cell lines [EV08, EV07] from CRISPRi data with over 10,000 perturbations per line, curated to at least 80% median knockdown [EV09]; as announced on 20 Aug 2026, the final test was released on 22 Oct and due on 5 Nov 2026 [EV08].

> 社区挑战赛检验的是同一个轴。Arc 的 2025 年挑战赛（H1 hESC 中的 300 个 CRISPRi 扰动）发现模型没有稳定地击败朴素基线，几乎所有模型在 MAE 上都不如基线，而获胜者把深度学习与统计特征和蛋白嵌入结合起来 [EV05]。2026 年挑战赛要求在六个未见细胞系中做零样本敲低预测 [EV08, EV07]，数据来自每个系超过 10,000 个扰动的 CRISPRi 实验，并筛选为敲低中位数至少 80% [EV09]；按 2026 年 8 月 20 日的公告，最终测试于 10 月 22 日发布，11 月 5 日截止 [EV08]。

Ancestry matters. In GEUVADIS, cross-population expression prediction into YRI reaches a mean R2 of 0.062 to 0.084 against 0.103 to 0.137 among European groups [EV23]; GTEx v8 has up to 15% non-European donors [EV25]; portability falls by over 32% when the causal variant is common in Europeans and rarer in Africans (abstract level) [EV24]; European-trained models predict better in European than African American donors, with per-tissue gaps that flip sign in some tissues [EV26]; and portability metrics disagree, with power a major confounder (abstract level) [EV27].

> 祖源很重要。在 GEUVADIS 中，对 YRI 的跨人群表达预测平均 R2 为 0.062 至 0.084，而欧洲各组之间为 0.103 至 0.137 [EV23]；GTEx v8 最多有 15% 的非欧洲供体 [EV25]；当因果变异在欧洲人中常见而在非洲人中较罕见时，可移植性下降超过 32%（摘要层面）[EV24]；欧洲训练的模型在欧洲供体中的预测好于非裔美国供体，各组织的差距在某些组织中会翻转符号 [EV26]；可移植性指标相互不一致，效能是主要的混杂因素（摘要层面）[EV27]。

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J5 (confidence: high for the evaluation claim). Evaluation design matters more than architecture. The drug main effect is expected to be much larger than the genotype-by-drug interaction, and genome-wide MSE or Pearson correlation is dominated by mean baselines [EV01, EV02]. What is needed: difference-in-differences between genotypes, rank and distributional metrics, positive and negative controls such as genotype permutation and benign variants, a perturbed-centroid reference, empirical upper bounds, a mechanism-fidelity check, downstream-utility benchmarks with lineage-grouped splits, and separate splits for unseen context, unseen perturbation, unseen dataset and unseen genotype [EV29, EV31, EV33, EV34, EV35].

> J5（置信度：对评测这一论断为高）。评测设计比架构更重要。预计药物主效应远大于基因型×药物交互，全基因组 MSE 或 Pearson 相关由均值基线主导 [EV01, EV02]。所需要的是：基因型之间的双重差分、排名和分布层面的指标、正负对照（如基因型置换和良性变异）、扰动后质心参照、经验上限、机制保真度检查、使用按谱系分组划分的下游效用基准，以及对未见背景、未见扰动、未见数据集和未见基因型分别划分 [EV29, EV31, EV33, EV34, EV35]。

J9 (confidence: medium). Ancestry should be a first-class covariate with stratified evaluation, given the European bias of eQTL and expression-prediction resources [EV23, EV24, EV26]. Ancestry effects on the response to infection are pervasive but cell-type specific (abstract level), which also supports a cell-type-resolved genotype covariate [GD49].

> J9（置信度：中）。鉴于 eQTL 和表达预测资源偏向欧洲人，祖源应当作为一等协变量并分层评测 [EV23, EV24, EV26]。祖源对感染响应的影响是普遍的但具细胞类型特异性（摘要层面），这也支持使用细胞类型分辨的基因型协变量 [GD49]。

J16 (confidence: medium). Statistical power is likely the binding constraint on any genotype contrast in a 50-line panel: the smaller arm of the 12 lineage-matched contrasts computed holds 1 to 6 lines (derived, Q137), so every result should carry an uncertainty estimate and a power statement [DS07].

> J16（置信度：中）。统计效能很可能是 50 个细胞系的面板上任何基因型对比的约束：已计算的 12 个按谱系匹配的对比中，较小的一臂只有 1 至 6 个系（推导，Q137），所以每个结果都应附上不确定性估计和效能说明 [DS07]。

J27 (confidence: medium). A genotype-aware model should be tested on unseen-genotype splits and judged for transportability. For a variant that changes the mechanism, failure to transport to other contexts is expected, and passing an unseen-context split does not imply passing an unseen-genotype split [EV32, EV35].

> J27（置信度：中）。基因型感知模型应当在未见基因型划分上检验，并评判其可迁移性。对改变机制的变异，在其他背景下迁移失败是预期之中的，通过未见背景划分并不意味着能通过未见基因型划分 [EV32, EV35]。

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Fix the evaluation protocol before any model is built, using the checklist above, and publish it with the baselines so that results can be compared across experiments [EV02, EV30].

> 在构建任何模型之前确定评测方案，使用上面的清单，并与基线一起公布，以便跨实验比较结果 [EV02, EV30]。

Add a positive control that has a known genotype-by-drug effect, for example the response QTLs of the anthracycline study, and a negative control built by permuting genotype labels, so that the evaluation can tell a real gain from leakage [GD06].

> 加入一个具有已知基因型×药物效应的阳性对照（例如蒽环类研究的响应 QTL），以及一个由置换基因型标签构成的阴性对照，使评测能区分真实增益与泄漏 [GD06]。

Adopt the perturbed-centroid reference and the average-effect baseline (the drug effect averaged over genotypes) in the interaction metric, calibrate the metric with null and ceiling baselines before comparing models, and implement centroid accuracy next to the delta-profile correlation; settle the licence before copying code from the Systema repository, because it states none [EV29, EV30, EV31, EV36].

> 在交互指标中采用扰动后质心参照和平均效应基线（跨基因型平均的药物效应），在比较模型之前用零基线和上限基线校准该指标，并在 delta 谱相关之外实现质心准确度；在复制 Systema 仓库的代码之前先确定许可，因为它没有声明任何许可 [EV29, EV30, EV31, EV36]。

Add a per-genotype mechanism-fidelity check and a downstream drug-response utility check with lineage-grouped splits to the harness [EV33, EV34], and score unseen-drug predictors with activity-weighted metrics on scaffold-held-out splits [EV38].

> 在评测框架中加入逐基因型的机制保真度检查，以及使用按谱系分组划分的下游药物响应效用检查 [EV33, EV34]，并对未见药物预测器在按骨架留出的划分上使用活性加权指标评分 [EV38]。

## 7. Evidence gaps / 证据缺口

No benchmark targeting the genotype-by-drug interaction was found in the evaluation searches (Q63, Q78, Q79, Q80, Q85, Q98, Q113); the authors of the OP3 benchmark note that existing datasets lack genetic-background variation [EV14]. The design of the 2026 challenge was read from the organizers' pages, while the Cell article was read at abstract level only [EV07]. The pitfalls section of PerturBench was not retrieved (Q32).

> 在评测相关的检索中没有找到针对基因型×药物交互的基准（Q63、Q78、Q79、Q80、Q85、Q98、Q113）；OP3 基准的作者指出现有数据集缺少遗传背景的变异 [EV14]。2026 年挑战赛的设计读自主办方的页面，而 Cell 文章只读到摘要层面 [EV07]。PerturBench 的陷阱部分没有取回（Q32）。
