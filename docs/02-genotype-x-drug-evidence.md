# 02 Genotype × drug → transcriptome evidence / 基因型×药物→转录组实证

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document collects the empirical evidence that genotype changes how cells respond to a drug at the transcriptome level: response-eQTL and gene-by-environment studies, dynamic and single-cell eQTL work, functional screens that read variant effects through the transcriptome, and the somatic-mutation literature on adaptive rewiring and resistance. It is the core evidence document for the question, and its main finding is that the evidence exists but is thin.

> 本文档汇集基因型在转录组层面改变细胞对药物响应的实证证据：响应型 eQTL 与基因×环境研究、动态与单细胞 eQTL 工作、通过转录组读出变异效应的功能筛选，以及关于适应性重编程和耐药的体细胞突变文献。它是本课题的核心证据文档，其主要发现是：证据存在，但很单薄。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

Response-eQTL (reQTL) studies exist, but each is small. Doxorubicin-treated iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes from 45 individuals yielded 376 reQTLs and 42 response sQTLs, and more than 6,000 genes were differentially spliced [GD06]. 114 lymphoblastoid lines treated with glucocorticoid showed genotype-by-treatment interactions for 26 genes, including NQO1, AIRE and SGK1, and for IL6 secretion, and genotype effects could appear only under treatment or only under control [GD08]. Primary hepatocytes from African American donors treated with several drugs reported 2,988 reQTLs, but the donor and drug counts were not retrieved (Q01) [GD01].

> 响应型 eQTL（reQTL）研究确实存在，但每项规模都不大。来自 45 人的 iPSC 来源心肌细胞经阿霉素处理，得到 376 个 reQTL 和 42 个响应 sQTL，超过 6,000 个基因发生差异剪接 [GD06]。114 个淋巴母细胞系经糖皮质激素处理，26 个基因（包括 NQO1、AIRE 和 SGK1）以及 IL6 分泌存在基因型×处理交互，且基因型效应可以只出现在处理下或只出现在对照下 [GD08]。非裔供体的原代肝细胞经多种药物处理，报告 2,988 个 reQTL，但供体数与药物数未取得（Q01）[GD01]。

The doxorubicin study is the only one retrieved that connects in-vitro response to clinical risk: inter-individual differences in transcriptional response predicted in-vitro cell damage, and that damage was associated with in-vivo anthracycline cardiotoxicity risk [GD06]. A separate study of iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes from 6 women found a shared gene-expression response signature across anthracyclines [GD07].

> 阿霉素研究是检索到的唯一把体外响应与临床风险联系起来的研究：转录响应的个体间差异预测了体外细胞损伤，而这种损伤与体内蒽环类药物的心脏毒性风险相关 [GD06]。另一项针对 6 名女性 iPSC 来源心肌细胞的研究发现了蒽环类药物之间共享的基因表达响应特征 [GD07]。

A summary of related work states that treatment-specific interactions between the cellular environment and cis-variants were relatively rare, with one validated glucocorticoid-specific effect on TNC. The search output itself noted a separate osteoblast study, so it is unclear whether that statement belongs to [GD08] or to another study.

> 一份相关工作的总结称，细胞环境与 cis 变异之间的处理特异交互相对少见，并验证了一个糖皮质激素特异的 TNC 效应。检索输出本身提示另有一项成骨细胞研究，因此尚不清楚这一说法属于 [GD08] 还是另一项研究。

eQTL effects vary with cell state, stimulus and differentiation stage. 125 iPSC lines profiled at 4 time points of endoderm differentiation gave about 899 time-dynamic eQTLs according to a secondary summary [GD18], and a method for single-cell allele-specific expression, applied to the same iPSC differentiation data, found 657 dynamically regulated genes, again from a secondary summary [GD19]. T-cell-state-dependent eQTLs [GD20] and lineage-specific dynamic regulation during cardiomyocyte differentiation [GD21] have also been reported. The single-cell eQTLGen consortium describes dynamic eQTLs along pseudotime and notes that regulatory-network changes may explain part of the inter-individual variation in drug response, a point it says has not been studied in detail [GD14].

> eQTL 的效应随细胞状态、刺激和分化阶段而变化。125 株 iPSC 在内胚层分化的 4 个时间点被测定，据二手总结约有 899 个时间动态 eQTL [GD18]，一种单细胞等位基因特异表达方法应用于同一套 iPSC 分化数据，发现 657 个动态调控基因，同样来自二手总结 [GD19]。也已有 T 细胞状态依赖 eQTL [GD20] 以及心肌细胞分化过程中谱系特异动态调控 [GD21] 的报道。单细胞 eQTLGen 联盟描述了沿拟时间变化的动态 eQTL，并指出调控网络的变化可能解释药物反应个体间差异的一部分，但它称这一点尚未被详细研究 [GD14]。

Stimuli change both the mean and the variability of gene expression, and genetic variants have been identified for both [GD09]. Most context-specific eQTLs can already be detected in very large bulk RNA-seq data, but single cells are needed to pinpoint the relevant context [GD16].

> 刺激会同时改变基因表达的均值和变异度，并且已鉴定出针对两者的遗传变异 [GD09]。多数情境特异的 eQTL 已经可以在超大规模 bulk RNA-seq 数据中检出，但需要单细胞才能定位相关情境 [GD16]。

Allele-specific expression offers a second way to test genotype-by-environment effects. EAGLE applies a binomial generalized linear mixed model to allele-specific counts while controlling for environment-independent cis-eQTL effects; applied to 922 individuals of the Depression Genes and Networks cohort, it found 35 significant associations at a false discovery rate of 10% (bioRxiv preprint 2016, search-summary level), although the cohort's environments are not drug treatments [GD12]. A separate study links SNPs in transcription-factor binding sites to drug-response variation in lymphoblastoid cell lines, on the premise that such SNPs modulate the factor's regulatory activity (search-summary level; the journal is unverified) [GD02].

> 等位基因特异表达提供了检验基因型×环境效应的第二种方式。EAGLE 对等位基因特异计数使用二项广义线性混合模型，同时控制与环境无关的 cis-eQTL 效应；应用于 Depression Genes and Networks 队列的 922 名个体，在 10% 的错误发现率下发现 35 个显著关联（2016 年 bioRxiv 预印本，检索摘要层面），但该队列的环境并非药物处理 [GD12]。另一项研究把转录因子结合位点中的 SNP 与淋巴母细胞系中药物响应的变异联系起来，前提是这类 SNP 会调节该因子的调控活性（检索摘要层面；期刊未经核实）[GD02]。

Functional screens that read variant effects at transcriptome level already exist. Base editing plus Perturb-seq was used for drug-resistance variants in 11 cancer genes (dabrafenib and cetuximab, HT-29 cells), and a sensitizing EGFR variant was validated [GD23]. Perturb(BE)-seq was used on GATA1 variants and fetal-hemoglobin promoter variants [GD24], and Perturb-seq has scored TP53 and KRAS coding variants [GD26]. Base editing keeps variants at endogenous expression levels but editing is incomplete, and editing efficiency depends on local sequence context [GD27, GD28].

> 在转录组层面读取变异效应的功能筛选已经存在。碱基编辑加 Perturb-seq 被用于 11 个癌症基因中的耐药变异（dabrafenib 和 cetuximab，HT-29 细胞），并验证了一个使 EGFR 敏化的变异 [GD23]。Perturb(BE)-seq 被用于 GATA1 变异和胎儿血红蛋白启动子变异 [GD24]，Perturb-seq 也对 TP53 和 KRAS 编码变异进行了打分 [GD26]。碱基编辑使变异保持在内源表达水平，但编辑不完全，且编辑效率取决于局部序列背景 [GD27, GD28]。

I did not find human data that combine several donor genotypes, Perturb-seq and drugs (Q33, Q60, Q116). The closest analogue is a yeast CRISPRi study across 169 segregants, in which 460 genes showed background-dependent perturbation effects, with 234 interacting loci and 4 hub loci [GD30]. The human perturbation panels retrieved here mostly use a single donor background: the iPSC-cardiomyocyte panel had 75 perturbation conditions [GD31], and the genome-wide iPSC CRISPRi atlas used one line [DS09]. The genome-scale screen in primary CD4+ T cells used 4 donors, but no donor-by-perturbation analysis was seen in the retrieved excerpt [DS08].

> 我没有找到同时包含多个供体基因型、Perturb-seq 和药物的人类数据（Q33、Q60、Q116）。最接近的类比是跨 169 个分离子的酵母 CRISPRi 研究，其中 460 个基因的扰动效应依赖遗传背景，涉及 234 个交互位点和 4 个枢纽位点 [GD30]。此处检索到的人类扰动面板大多使用单一供体背景：iPSC 心肌细胞面板有 75 种扰动条件 [GD31]，全基因组 iPSC CRISPRi 图谱只用了一个细胞系 [DS09]。原代 CD4+ T 细胞的基因组规模筛选使用了 4 名供体，但在检索到的摘录中未见供体×扰动的交互分析 [DS08]。

The cell-type specificity of drug-induced transcription depends on drug class. In about 600 LINCS drug–cell-line pairs, inhibitors of signaling kinases gave cell-type-specific changes, whereas inhibitors of chaperones and cell-cycle kinases gave more uniform changes [GD41].

> 药物诱导转录的细胞类型特异性取决于药物类别。在约 600 个 LINCS 药物-细胞系对中，信号激酶抑制剂引起细胞类型特异的变化，而伴侣蛋白和细胞周期激酶抑制剂引起的变化较一致 [GD41]。

Somatic genotype shapes adaptive transcriptional rewiring. In BRAF-mutant melanoma, resistance was associated with BRAF V600E allele zygosity and receptor-tyrosine-kinase mutation status, with broad ErbB up-regulation after treatment [GD42]. Resistance to BRAF and EGFR inhibition in BRAF V600E colorectal tumors runs through an SRC–COX2 program that is reversible [GD32], and EGFR expression rose in 6 of 16 post-resistance melanoma biopsies [GD36]. Two TP53 wild-type colorectal lines responded differently to BRAF V600E expression [GD44], and mutant p53 shaped chromatin accessibility and drug tolerance in pancreatic cancer [GD43].

> 体细胞基因型塑造适应性转录重编程。在 BRAF 突变黑色素瘤中，耐药与 BRAF V600E 等位基因合子性及受体酪氨酸激酶突变状态相关，治疗后 ErbB 通路广泛上调 [GD42]。BRAF V600E 结直肠肿瘤对 BRAF 和 EGFR 抑制的耐药经由一个可逆的 SRC–COX2 程序 [GD32]，16 例耐药后黑色素瘤活检中有 6 例 EGFR 表达上升 [GD36]。两株 TP53 野生型结直肠细胞系对 BRAF V600E 表达的反应不同 [GD44]，突变型 p53 塑造了胰腺癌中的染色质可及性和药物耐受 [GD43]。

The search summaries suggest that non-genetic transcriptional adaptation often precedes genetic resistance; this is a synthesis across sources, not a single-paper result [GD33, GD34, GD37]. Different inhibitor classes give different kinome-reprogramming patterns: BRAF–MEK–ERK and PI3K–AKT inhibitors differ [GD34]. At title level only, a mechanistic model of MAPK signaling is reported to show how allostery and network rewiring contribute to drug resistance [GD35].

> 检索摘要提示，非遗传的转录适应常早于遗传耐药；这是跨来源的综合，不是单篇论文的结果 [GD33, GD34, GD37]。不同类别的抑制剂产生不同的激酶组重编程模式：BRAF–MEK–ERK 与 PI3K–AKT 抑制剂不同 [GD34]。仅在标题层面，有一个 MAPK 信号的机理模型被报告用来说明变构和网络重连如何促成耐药 [GD35]。

Single-cell response-eQTL data now exist at the scale of tens of individuals. Cell cultures derived from stem cells from 51 individuals were exposed to nicotine, caffeine or ethanol and profiled by single-cell RNA-seq (about 1.4 million cells); they gave 31 to 110 response eQTLs per cell type, with 380, 336 and 276 eGenes in the ethanol, caffeine and nicotine conditions, and the response eQTLs resembled disease risk loci more than steady-state eQTLs do [GD10]. A review describes the same study as using 53 individuals, whereas the paper reports 51 [GD10]. In 90 individuals, peripheral blood mononuclear cells exposed to influenza A virus or mock for 6 hours and profiled by single-cell RNA-seq with DNA sequencing showed genetic-ancestry effects on the response that were pervasive but cell-type specific and largely explained by cis-eQTLs [GD49].

> 单细胞响应 eQTL 数据现已达到数十个个体的规模。来自 51 名个体、由干细胞衍生的细胞培养物被施加尼古丁、咖啡因或乙醇处理，并做单细胞 RNA-seq（约 1.4 million（140 万）个细胞）；每种细胞类型有 31 至 110 个响应 eQTL，乙醇、咖啡因和尼古丁条件下分别有 380、336 和 276 个 eGene，响应 eQTL 比稳态 eQTL 更像疾病风险位点 [GD10]。一篇综述称同一研究使用了 53 名个体，而论文报告的是 51 名 [GD10]。在 90 名个体中，外周血单个核细胞暴露于甲型流感病毒或对照 6 小时，并做单细胞 RNA-seq 和 DNA 测序，结果显示祖源对响应的影响普遍存在但具细胞类型特异性，且大部分可由 cis-eQTL 解释 [GD49]。

Pooled culture is a low-cost route to donor-by-perturbation designs. A village of 104 hiPSC donors was profiled by single-cell RNA-seq (86,185 cells) after demultiplexing by genotype, and neural progenitor villages were used to map natural variation in expression and in viral susceptibility, although pooled composition and cross-line signalling can confound a drug response [GD47, GD48]. A reporter-assay study of caffeine-treated vascular endothelial cells tested more than 43,000 variants; 665 had allelic effects and 6 changed the response to caffeine, about 1% [GD50].

> 混池培养是实现供体×扰动设计的低成本途径。一个由 104 名供体 hiPSC 组成的村落在按基因型解复用后做了单细胞 RNA-seq（86,185 个细胞），神经祖细胞村落被用来绘制表达和病毒易感性的自然变异，但混池的构成和细胞系间信号可能混杂药物响应 [GD47, GD48]。一项咖啡因处理的血管内皮细胞的报告基因研究检测了 43,000 多个变异；665 个有等位基因效应，6 个改变了对咖啡因的响应，约占 1% [GD50]。

Somatic variants can switch a drug-induced programme off. In a pool of cell lines demultiplexed by genotype, each of 7 TP53 wild-type lines showed a robust response whereas TP53-mutant lines showed only minimal change [GD45]. Isogenic MCF10A lines (51 stable lines expressing wild-type or mutant cancer genes) screened with 90 compounds give variant-by-drug labels in one background, although the readout is proliferation, not transcription [GD46], and a CRISPR-drug map of 18 cell lines and 8 drugs gives pathway-gene-by-drug labels with a growth readout [GD53]. Coding-variant maps for BCR-ABL1 are described in document 01 [GV28, GV29, GV30].

> 体细胞变异可以把药物诱导的程序关闭。在按基因型解复用的细胞系混池中，7 个 TP53 野生型细胞系各自都显示出稳健的响应，而 TP53 突变细胞系只有极小的变化 [GD45]。同基因的 MCF10A 细胞系（表达野生型或突变型癌基因的 51 个稳定细胞系）用 90 种化合物筛选，在单一背景下给出变异×药物标签，但读出是增殖而不是转录 [GD46]；涵盖 18 个细胞系和 8 种药物的 CRISPR–药物图谱给出以生长为读出的通路基因×药物标签 [GD53]。BCR-ABL1 的编码变异图谱见文档 01 [GV28, GV29, GV30]。

Baseline heterogeneity predisposes the response even within one genotype. In melanoma cells treated with the BRAF inhibitor vemurafenib, rare cells (1 in 50 to 1 in 500) that already express resistance markers before treatment are reprogrammed epigenetically into a stably resistant state [GD51]. Variant-specific in-vitro responses transport to the clinic qualitatively rather than quantitatively: forskolin-induced swelling of patient-derived organoids separates responders from non-responders to CFTR modulators and guides the treatment of rare variants, but has not reliably predicted the size of the clinical response within genotypes [GD52].

> 即使在同一基因型内，基线异质性也决定响应倾向。在用 BRAF 抑制剂 vemurafenib 处理的黑色素瘤细胞中，稀有细胞（1 in 50 到 1 in 500）在处理前就表达耐药标志物，随后经表观遗传重编程为稳定耐药状态 [GD51]。变异特异的体外响应能定性地、而不能定量地迁移到临床：患者来源类器官的 forskolin 诱导肿胀实验能区分 CFTR 调节剂的应答者与非应答者并指导罕见变异的治疗，但尚不能可靠预测同一基因型内的临床应答幅度 [GD52]。

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J1 (revised; confidence: medium). Data that pair inherited genotype, an exposure and a single-cell transcriptome do exist, but at the scale of tens of individuals: about 51 individuals with three compounds [GD10] and 90 donors with one virus [GD49]. Larger donor-by-perturbation grids also exist (12 donors with 90 cytokines [DS16], 40 donors with 11 cytokines [MD42]), but the retrieved text does not say whether their donors are genotyped. No public data pair genotype with many drugs at a scale suitable for training deep models: published reQTL studies cover roughly 45–114 individuals and few drugs, and cell-line panels offer only about 50–70 genotypes confounded with lineage and epigenome [GD06, GD08, DS01, DS05]. The earlier version of this judgment said that no such data were found, which was too strong; absence in a limited search engine remains weak evidence (queries Q57, Q60, Q76 and Q116).

> J1（修订；置信度：中）。同时包含遗传性基因型、暴露和单细胞转录组的数据确实存在，但规模在数十个个体：约 51 个个体、3 种化合物 [GD10]，以及 90 名供体、1 种病毒 [GD49]。也存在更大的供体×扰动网格（12 名供体、90 种细胞因子 [DS16]；40 名供体、11 种细胞因子 [MD42]），但检索到的文字没有说明其供体是否做了基因分型。没有公开数据在适合训练深度模型的规模上把基因型与多种药物配对：已发表的 reQTL 研究约覆盖 45–114 人且药物很少，细胞系面板只提供约 50–70 个与谱系和表观基因组混杂的基因型 [GD06, GD08, DS01, DS05]。此判断的早先版本称没有找到此类数据，这一说法过强；在受限搜索引擎中的缺失仍然只是弱证据（查询 Q57、Q60、Q76 和 Q116）。

J2 (confidence: medium). The most trainable slice is probably somatic driver mutations crossed with targeted drugs in cancer cell lines: effects are larger, annotations are complete, and data are abundant [DS01, DS05, GD42]. Common germline variants have small effects and are better used for external validation and as priors.

> J2（置信度：中）。最可训练的切片很可能是癌症细胞系中的体细胞驱动突变 × 靶向药物：效应更大，注释完整，数据丰富 [DS01, DS05, GD42]。常见的种系变异效应小，更适合用作外部验证和先验。

J3 (confidence: medium; the key testable hypothesis). Most genotype effects on drug-induced expression change may be mediated through the baseline transcriptional state. If so, a model conditioned on control expression already absorbs genotype implicitly, and explicit variant features help only when they change the response operator itself, through binding sites, gain or loss of function, or response-specific regulatory variants. The first experiment should therefore test whether genotype adds information beyond baseline expression; the only supporting hint is indirect [EV16].

> J3（置信度：中；关键的可检验假设）。基因型对药物诱导表达变化的大部分影响，可能是经由基线转录状态中介的。若如此，已条件于对照表达的模型就已隐式吸收了基因型，只有当变异通过结合位点、功能获得或丧失、或响应特异的调控变异改变响应算子本身时，显式变异特征才有帮助。因此第一个实验应检验基因型是否在基线表达之外提供额外信息；唯一的支持线索是间接的 [EV16]。

J17 (confidence: medium). Mediation through the baseline state and a change of the response operator coexist, so J3 and hypothesis HA should be stratified by variant class. Response eQTLs are regulatory, sparse and cell-type specific [GD10, GD49, GD50], which favors baseline mediation for expression-level (class 2) variants, whereas TP53 status [GD45] and BCR-ABL1 resistance [GV28, GV29, GV30] change the operator itself and favor explicit variant modeling for classes 1 and 4. Baseline cell-state heterogeneity also predisposes the response within one genotype [GD51], so the target should be a distribution of cell states rather than a population mean.

> J17（置信度：中）。经由基线状态的中介与响应算子的变化并存，因此 J3 和假设 HA 应按变异类别分层。响应 eQTL 是调控性的、稀疏的、具细胞类型特异性 [GD10, GD49, GD50]，这支持表达水平（类别 2）变异的基线中介，而 TP53 状态 [GD45] 和 BCR-ABL1 耐药 [GV28, GV29, GV30] 改变的是算子本身，支持对类别 1 和 4 做显式变异建模。基线细胞状态的异质性也决定同一基因型内的响应倾向 [GD51]，所以目标应当是细胞状态的分布，而不是群体均值。

J18 (confidence: low-medium). Variant-specific in-vitro responses should be expected to transport to the clinic qualitatively, not quantitatively. Organoid swelling separates responders from non-responders to CFTR modulators but has not reliably predicted the size of the response within a genotype [GD52], so a model that is accurate in vitro cannot be assumed to give calibrated clinical effect sizes.

> J18（置信度：低中）。应当预期变异特异的体外响应能定性地、而不能定量地迁移到临床。类器官肿胀实验能区分 CFTR 调节剂的应答者与非应答者，但尚不能可靠预测同一基因型内的应答幅度 [GD52]，因此不能假定在体外准确的模型能给出经过校准的临床效应大小。

J28 (confidence: low-medium). Two mechanism-level routes complement response-eQTL mapping for expression-level (class 2) variants. Allele-specific expression conditions on each individual's own alleles and tests whether genotype modulates a response in cis without testing candidate SNPs one by one [GD12]. A transcription-factor-centered explanation, in which a SNP in a binding site changes the factor's regulatory activity and thereby the drug response, could be tested with a TF-activity readout [GD02, KB20]. Neither route has been shown here on drug-induced transcriptomes: the cohort environments of the first are not treatments [GD12], and the second rests on a search-summary description [GD02].

> J28（置信度：低中）。对表达水平（类别 2）的变异，有两条机理层面的途径可补充响应 eQTL 定位。等位基因特异表达以每个个体自身的等位基因为条件，无需逐个检验候选 SNP，即可检验基因型是否以 cis 方式调节响应 [GD12]。以转录因子为中心的解释——结合位点中的 SNP 改变该因子的调控活性，进而改变药物响应——可用 TF 活性读出来检验 [GD02, KB20]。这两条途径都还没有在药物诱导的转录组上被证明：前者队列中的环境并非处理 [GD12]，后者只基于检索摘要层面的描述 [GD02]。

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Treat the somatic track as primary, and use published reQTL results, such as the anthracycline study, as external validation and positive controls for the germline track [GD06, DS13].

> 把体细胞轨道作为主要轨道，并把已发表的 reQTL 结果（如蒽环类药物研究）用作种系轨道的外部验证和阳性对照 [GD06, DS13]。

For every study added later, record the same fields so that studies can be compared: genotype source, drug, dose, time, cell type, readout and number of individuals or lines.

> 对以后加入的每项研究都记录相同的字段，以便比较各研究：基因型来源、药物、剂量、时间、细胞类型、读出方式以及个体数或细胞系数。

Run the mediation test described in document 08 before investing in explicit variant modeling.

> 在投入显式变异建模之前，先运行文档 08 中描述的中介检验。

Use the response-eQTL cultures [GD10] and the influenza-exposed PBMCs [GD49] as positive controls for the mediation test in the germline-like setting, and check whether the donors of the large cytokine datasets are genotyped before using them for genotype questions [DS16, MD42].

> 在类种系情形下，把响应 eQTL 培养 [GD10] 和流感暴露的 PBMC [GD49] 用作中介检验的阳性对照，并在把大型细胞因子数据集用于基因型问题之前，核查其供体是否做了基因分型 [DS16, MD42]。

For the somatic track, use genotype-demultiplexed pooled designs [GD45] and the BCR-ABL1 maps [GV28, GV29, GV30] as label sources, and record for each whether the readout is a transcriptome or only growth.

> 对体细胞轨道，把按基因型解复用的混池设计 [GD45] 和 BCR-ABL1 图谱 [GV28, GV29, GV30] 用作标签来源，并为每一项记录读出是转录组还是仅有生长。
