# 02 Genotype × drug → transcriptome evidence / 基因型×药物→转录组实证

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document collects the empirical evidence that genotype changes how cells respond to a drug at the transcriptome level: response-eQTL and gene-by-environment studies, dynamic and single-cell eQTL work, functional screens that read variant effects through the transcriptome, and the somatic-mutation literature on adaptive rewiring and resistance. Its main finding is that the evidence exists but is thin.

> 本文档收集基因型在转录组层面改变细胞对药物响应的实证：响应 eQTL 和基因×环境研究、动态和单细胞 eQTL 工作、通过转录组读出变异效应的功能筛选，以及关于适应性重编程和耐药的体细胞突变文献。它的主要发现是：证据存在，但很薄。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

Bulk response-eQTL studies are small. Doxorubicin-treated iPSC cardiomyocytes from 45 individuals gave reQTLs for 376 genes (447 with allele-specific expression) and 42 response sQTLs, and response eQTLs, unlike baseline eQTLs, enriched in cardiotoxicity GWAS signal [GD06]. In 114 lymphoblastoid lines, dexamethasone changed 4,568 genes but only 26 carried a cis-eQTL-by-treatment interaction, against 6,813 with none [GD08]. Primary hepatocytes (60 cultures, 6 drugs) gave 2,988 response eQTLs, led by CYP3A5, a drug-metabolism gene (preprint abstract) [GD01]. Related drugs share responses: 93% of 142 doxorubicin response eGenes also responded to daunorubicin [GD07]. The only in-vitro to in-vivo link rests on a model trained on 3 versus 3 patients [GD06].

> 批量响应 eQTL 研究规模很小。阿霉素处理的 iPSC 心肌细胞来自 45 个个体，得到 376 个基因的 reQTL（加入等位基因特异表达后为 447 个）和 42 个响应 sQTL，且与基线 eQTL 不同，响应 eQTL 在心脏毒性 GWAS 信号中富集 [GD06]。在 114 个淋巴母细胞系中，地塞米松改变了 4,568 个基因，但只有 26 个基因带有 cis-eQTL×处理交互，而 6,813 个基因没有交互 [GD08]。原代肝细胞（60 个培养物、6 种药物）得到 2,988 个响应 eQTL，以药物代谢基因 CYP3A5 为首（预印本摘要）[GD01]。相关药物共享响应：142 个阿霉素响应 eGene 中有 93% 也对柔红霉素响应 [GD07]。唯一的体外到体内联系依赖于在 3 对 3 名患者上训练的模型 [GD06]。

Single-cell response-eQTL data now exist at the scale of tens to about a hundred individuals. Stem-cell-derived cultures from 51 individuals exposed to ethanol, caffeine or nicotine (1.4 million cells) gave 31 to 110 response eQTLs per cell type (380, 336 and 276 eGenes by exposure), and response eQTLs resembled disease loci more than baseline eQTLs (GWAS colocalization 4.8% versus 0.4%) [GD10]. In PBMCs, cell type mattered more than treatment: cell-type-specific eGene fractions (6% to 51%) exceeded treatment-specific ones (1% to 5%) in 96 children [GD09], and 20.9% of eQTL genes were response eQTLs in 120 individuals, following cell type and time more than pathogen [GD16]. Ancestry effects on the influenza response are pervasive, cell-type specific and largely cis-regulatory (abstract level) [GD49].

> 单细胞响应 eQTL 数据现在已有数十到约一百个个体的规模。来自 51 个个体、暴露于乙醇、咖啡因或尼古丁的干细胞来源培养物（1.4 百万，即 140 万个细胞）得到每种细胞类型 31 至 110 个响应 eQTL（按暴露分别有 380、336 和 276 个 eGene），且响应 eQTL 比基线 eQTL 更像疾病位点（GWAS 共定位 4.8% 对 0.4%）[GD10]。在 PBMC 中，细胞类型比处理更重要：在 96 名儿童中，细胞类型特异的 eGene 比例（6% 至 51%）超过处理特异的比例（1% 至 5%）[GD09]；在 120 个个体中，20.9% 的 eQTL 基因是响应 eQTL，其变化随细胞类型和时间多于随病原体 [GD16]。祖源对流感响应的影响是普遍的、细胞类型特异的，并且主要是顺式调控（摘要层面）[GD49]。

eQTL effects depend on cell state, not only on time or stimulus. Pooled differentiation of 125 genotype-demultiplexed iPSC lines gave 899 time-dynamic eQTLs among 4,422, with stages defined by pseudotime rather than collection day [GD18]; cardiomyocyte pseudotime recovered 357 dynamic-eQTL genes against 142 using collection day [GD21]; and continuous cell states mediated about one third of 6,511 cis-eQTLs in T cells (abstract level) [GD20]. Single-cell designs gain power from many donors with fewer cells each and from genetic multiplexing [GD14].

> eQTL 效应取决于细胞状态，而不仅仅取决于时间或刺激。125 个按基因型解复用的 iPSC 系的混池分化得到 4,422 个 eQTL 中的 899 个随时间动态变化，阶段由拟时间而不是采样日定义 [GD18]；心肌细胞拟时间找回了 357 个动态 eQTL 基因，而用采样日只有 142 个 [GD21]；连续的细胞状态介导了 T 细胞中约三分之一的 6,511 个 cis-eQTL（摘要层面）[GD20]。单细胞设计的功效来自许多供体各用较少细胞，以及遗传多路复用 [GD14]。

Functional screens read variant effects through the transcriptome, rarely with drugs. Base editing of 11 cancer genes (32,476 variants, ten drugs, four lines) defined four variant classes, and Perturb-seq in HT-29 cells showed that 22 of 35 resistance gRNAs changed the transcriptome, addiction variants most [GD23]. Perturb-seq of 200 TP53 and KRAS variants sorted them into graded classes (abstract level) [GD26], and 115 RUNX1 variants fell into WT-like, hypomorphic and LoF-like groups, without a drug arm [GD27]. The closest genotype-by-perturbation design is in yeast: 460 of 1,721 perturbed genes showed background-dependent effects across 169 segregants, with 157 of 234 interacting loci in four hubs [GD30]. Human atlases use one background (11,739 genes in one iPSC line [DS09]) or four donors (22 million CD4+ T cells [DS08]); no human study combining several donor genotypes, Perturb-seq and drugs was found (Q33, Q60, Q116).

> 功能筛选通过转录组读出变异效应，但很少带药物。对 11 个癌基因的碱基编辑（32,476 个变异、10 种药物、4 个细胞系）定义了四类变异，HT-29 细胞中的 Perturb-seq 显示 35 条耐药 gRNA 中有 22 条改变了转录组，以成瘾变异最多 [GD23]。对 200 个 TP53 和 KRAS 变异的 Perturb-seq 把它们分成分级的类别（摘要层面）[GD26]，115 个 RUNX1 变异分为类野生型、亚效和类功能丧失三组，没有药物组 [GD27]。最接近的基因型×扰动设计在酵母中：在 169 个分离子中，1,721 个被扰动基因里有 460 个表现出背景依赖的效应，234 个互作位点中有 157 个位于 4 个枢纽 [GD30]。人类图谱只用一个背景（一个 iPSC 系中的 11,739 个基因 [DS09]）或四名供体（2,200 万个 CD4+ T 细胞 [DS08]）；没有找到同时结合多个供体基因型、Perturb-seq 和药物的人类研究（Q33、Q60、Q116）。

Pooled designs lower the cost of donor- or line-by-perturbation data. Villages of 3 and 18 hiPSC lines were demultiplexed by SNPs, but line proportions drifted with growth rate (5 of 18 lines lost in 15 days), so drug-perturbed villages need composition tracking [GD47]. In a pool of 24 to 99 cancer lines with 13 drugs, nutlin moved all 7 TP53 wild-type lines and little in 17 TP53-mutant lines [GD45]. Growth-readout label sets add variant-by-drug labels in controlled backgrounds: 51 aberrant cancer genes by 90 drugs (abstract level) [GD46], 100 knockout lines [GD54] and 18 lines with 8 drugs [GD53]. In a caffeine reporter assay, 665 SNPs had allelic effects (false discovery rate below 10%) but only 6 of 15,927 tested variants had a conditional effect (below 5%) [GD50].

> 混池设计降低了供体或细胞系×扰动数据的成本。3 个和 18 个 hiPSC 系的村落按 SNP 解复用，但各系比例随生长速度漂移（15 天内 18 个系中有 5 个丢失），因此药物扰动的村落需要跟踪组成 [GD47]。在含 24 至 99 个癌症细胞系和 13 种药物的混池中，nutlin 使全部 7 个 TP53 野生型系发生变化，而在 17 个 TP53 突变系中变化很小 [GD45]。以生长为读出的标签集提供了受控背景下的变异×药物标签：51 个异常癌基因×90 种药物（摘要层面）[GD46]、100 个敲除系 [GD54] 以及 18 个系加 8 种药物 [GD53]。在咖啡因报告基因实验中，665 个 SNP 有等位基因效应（FDR 低于 10%），但 15,927 个被检测变异中只有 6 个有条件性效应（低于 5%）[GD50]。

Somatic genotype shapes adaptive transcriptional rewiring, but cell state matters within one genotype. Among 12 BRAF V600E melanoma lines, only a four-line group showed broad ErbB up-regulation [GD42]; a reversible SRC–COX2 programme drives resistance in BRAF V600E colorectal lines [GD32]; and BRAF V600E moved the proliferation of two TP53 wild-type colorectal lines in opposite directions [GD44]. Adaptation unfolds from minutes to days [GD33] and can be too fast for selected mutations [GD37]. Rare pre-resistant melanoma cells (1 in 50 to 1 in 500) are reprogrammed by the drug into a stable resistant state [GD51], and four persister states recur under one BRAF V600E genotype with no mutation shared across six relapses [GD55]. Signaling-kinase inhibitors give cell-type-specific signatures, whereas chaperone and cell-cycle kinase inhibitors give uniform ones [GD41].

> 体细胞基因型塑造适应性转录重编程，但在同一基因型内细胞状态同样重要。在 12 个 BRAF V600E 黑色素瘤细胞系中，只有一个含四个系的组表现出广泛的 ErbB 上调 [GD42]；一条可逆的 SRC–COX2 程序驱动 BRAF V600E 结直肠细胞系的耐药 [GD32]；BRAF V600E 使两个 TP53 野生型结直肠细胞系的增殖朝相反方向变化 [GD44]。适应过程从数分钟展开到数天 [GD33]，并且可能快到不能由被选择的突变来解释 [GD37]。稀有的、预先存在耐药倾向的黑色素瘤细胞（1:50 至 1:500）被药物重编程为稳定的耐药状态 [GD51]，并且在同一 BRAF V600E 基因型下有四种持续存活状态反复出现，六次复发之间没有共享的突变 [GD55]。信号激酶抑制剂给出细胞类型特异的特征，而伴侣蛋白和细胞周期激酶抑制剂给出一致的特征 [GD41]。

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J1 (confidence: medium). Data that pair inherited genotype, an exposure and a single-cell transcriptome exist, but at the scale of tens to about a hundred individuals: 51 individuals with three compounds [GD10], 96 children with five conditions [GD09] and 120 individuals with three pathogens [GD16]. Larger donor-by-perturbation grids have no stated genotypes (12 donors with 90 cytokines [DS16], 40 donors reported for Stack [MD42]). No public data pair genotype with many drugs at a scale suitable for training deep models: bulk reQTL studies cover roughly 45 to 114 individuals and few drugs [GD06, GD08, GD01], and cell-line panels offer 50 genotypes confounded with lineage [DS07]. Absence in a limited search engine is weak evidence (Q57, Q60, Q76, Q116).

> J1（置信度：中）。把遗传性基因型、暴露和单细胞转录组配对的数据存在，但只有数十到约一百个个体的规模：51 个个体加三种化合物 [GD10]、96 名儿童加五种条件 [GD09]、120 个个体加三种病原体 [GD16]。更大的供体×扰动网格没有说明基因型（12 名供体、90 种细胞因子 [DS16]；Stack 报告的 40 名供体 [MD42]）。没有公开数据以适合训练深度模型的规模把基因型与多种药物配对：批量 reQTL 研究涵盖约 45 至 114 个个体和很少的药物 [GD06, GD08, GD01]，细胞系面板只提供与谱系混杂的 50 种基因型 [DS07]。在有限的检索引擎中没有找到只是弱证据（Q57、Q60、Q76、Q116）。

J2 (confidence: medium). The most trainable slice is probably somatic driver mutations crossed with targeted drugs in cancer cell lines: effects are larger, annotations are complete and data are abundant [DS07, DS01, GD42]. Common germline variants have small effects and are better used for external validation and as priors.

> J2（置信度：中）。最易训练的部分很可能是癌症细胞系中体细胞驱动突变与靶向药物的交叉：效应更大、注释完整、数据丰富 [DS07, DS01, GD42]。常见的种系变异效应很小，更适合用于外部验证和作为先验。

J3 (confidence: medium; the key testable hypothesis). Most genotype effects on drug-induced expression change may be mediated through the baseline transcriptional state. If so, a model conditioned on control expression already absorbs genotype implicitly, and explicit variant features help only when they change the response operator itself, through binding sites, gain or loss of function, or response-specific regulatory variants. The first experiment should therefore test whether genotype adds information beyond baseline expression; the only supporting hint is indirect [EV16].

> J3（置信度：中；关键的可检验假设）。基因型对药物诱导表达变化的大部分效应，可能是经由基线转录状态中介的。如果是这样，以对照表达为条件的模型已经隐式地吸收了基因型，只有当显式变异特征改变响应算子本身（经由结合位点、功能获得或丧失，或响应特异的调控变异）时才会有帮助。因此第一个实验应当检验基因型在基线表达之外是否增加信息；唯一的支持线索是间接的 [EV16]。

J17 (confidence: medium). Mediation through the baseline state and a change of the response operator coexist, so J3 and hypothesis HA should be stratified by variant class. Response eQTLs are regulatory, sparse and cell-type specific [GD10, GD49, GD50], which favors baseline mediation for expression-level (class 2) variants, whereas TP53 status [GD45] and BCR-ABL1 resistance [GV28, GV29, GV30] change the operator itself and favor explicit variant modeling for classes 1 and 4. Cell-state heterogeneity also predisposes the response within one genotype [GD51, GD55], so the target should be a distribution of cell states rather than a population mean.

> J17（置信度：中）。经由基线状态的中介与响应算子的改变并存，因此 J3 和假设 HA 应按变异类别分层。响应 eQTL 是调控性的、稀疏的、具细胞类型特异性 [GD10, GD49, GD50]，这有利于表达水平（类别 2）变异的基线中介；而 TP53 状态 [GD45] 和 BCR-ABL1 耐药 [GV28, GV29, GV30] 改变的是算子本身，有利于对类别 1 和 4 做显式变异建模。细胞状态的异质性也使同一基因型内的响应具有倾向性 [GD51, GD55]，所以目标应当是细胞状态的分布，而不是群体均值。

J18 (confidence: low-medium). In-vitro to in-vivo transfer is supported qualitatively for class-1 growth labels, where saturation screens matched 28 of 29 acquired-resistance cases for FGFR [GV33], but the only transcriptional-response link rests on 3 versus 3 patients [GD06]; calibrated clinical effect sizes should not be assumed from an accurate in-vitro model.

> J18（置信度：低中）。体外到体内的迁移在定性上得到类别 1 生长标签的支持：对 FGFR，饱和突变筛选与 29 个获得性耐药病例中的 28 个相符 [GV33]，但唯一的转录响应联系只依赖 3 对 3 名患者 [GD06]；不应由一个体外准确的模型就假定得到经过校准的临床效应大小。

J28 (confidence: low-medium). Two mechanism-level routes complement response-eQTL mapping for expression-level (class 2) variants. Allele-specific expression conditions on each individual's own alleles and tests genotype-by-environment effects without testing SNPs one by one: it found 35 associations at a 10% false discovery rate in 922 blood samples against 4 by standard interaction testing, though the environments are not drug treatments [GD12]. A transcription-factor-centered explanation, in which a binding-site SNP changes the factor's activity and thereby drug sensitivity, linked 334 of 3,864 TF-treatment pairs to cytotoxicity from basal expression [GD02] and could be tested with a TF-activity readout [KB20]; neither route has been shown on drug-induced transcriptomes.

> J28（置信度：低中）。对表达水平（类别 2）变异，有两条机理层面的途径可补充响应 eQTL 定位。等位基因特异表达以每个个体自身的等位基因为条件，无需逐个检验 SNP 就能检验基因型×环境效应：它在 922 个血液样本中以 10% 的 FDR 找到 35 个关联，而标准交互检验只找到 4 个，不过其环境并不是药物处理 [GD12]。以转录因子为中心的解释认为结合位点中的 SNP 改变转录因子的活性、进而改变药物敏感性，它利用基础表达把 3,864 个 TF-处理对中的 334 个与细胞毒性联系起来 [GD02]，并且可以用 TF 活性读出来检验 [KB20]；两条途径都没有在药物诱导的转录组上得到证明。

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Treat the somatic track as primary, and use published reQTL results, such as the anthracycline study, as external validation and positive controls for the germline track [GD06, DS13].

> 把体细胞轨道作为主要轨道，并把已发表的 reQTL 结果（如蒽环类研究）用作种系轨道的外部验证和阳性对照 [GD06, DS13]。

For every study added later, record the same fields so that studies can be compared: genotype source, drug, dose, time, cell type, readout and number of individuals or lines.

> 对之后加入的每项研究，记录相同的字段以便比较：基因型来源、药物、剂量、时间、细胞类型、读出以及个体或细胞系的数量。

Run the mediation test of document 08 before investing in explicit variant modeling, using the response-eQTL cultures [GD10] and the single-cell PBMC studies [GD09, GD16] as germline-like positive controls.

> 在投入显式变异建模之前，运行文档 08 的中介检验，并把响应 eQTL 培养物 [GD10] 和单细胞 PBMC 研究 [GD09, GD16] 用作类种系的阳性对照。

For the somatic track, use genotype-demultiplexed pooled designs [GD45] and the variant maps of document 01 as label sources, and record for each whether the readout is a transcriptome or only growth.

> 对体细胞轨道，把按基因型解复用的混池设计 [GD45] 和文档 01 的变异图谱用作标签来源，并记录每一项的读出是转录组还是仅有生长。

Check whether the donors of the large cytokine datasets are genotyped before using them for genotype questions [DS16, MD42].

> 在把大型细胞因子数据集用于基因型问题之前，核查其供体是否做了基因分型 [DS16, MD42]。

## 7. Evidence gaps / 证据缺口

No result on drug-stimulation-specific single-cell eQTLs was found (Q04), no human study combining several donor genotypes, Perturb-seq and drugs was found (Q12, Q33), no statin study in lymphoblastoid lines or hepatocytes was found (Q17, Q30) and no study stratifying BRAF, KRAS and TP53 status jointly with pharmacotranscriptomic readouts was found (Q43). The genotyping status of the donors of the large cytokine datasets is not stated [DS16, MD42].

> 没有找到关于药物刺激特异的单细胞 eQTL 的结果（Q04），没有找到同时结合多个供体基因型、Perturb-seq 和药物的人类研究（Q12、Q33），没有找到淋巴母细胞系或肝细胞中的他汀研究（Q17、Q30），也没有找到把 BRAF、KRAS 和 TP53 状态与药物转录组读出联合分层的研究（Q43）。大型细胞因子数据集供体的基因分型状态没有说明 [DS16, MD42]。
