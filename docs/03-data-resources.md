# 03 Data resources / 数据资源

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document describes the datasets that could feed a model of how variants shape drug-induced transcriptional responses: drug-perturbation transcriptomes, Perturb-seq atlases, response-QTL data and cell-line annotation, with their scale, genotype information and licences. It rests on dataset pages and cards (Q136) and on metadata counts derived for Tahoe-100M (Q137); no data files were downloaded.

> 本文档描述可用于建模“变异如何塑造药物诱导转录响应”的数据集：药物扰动转录组、Perturb-seq 图谱、响应 QTL 数据和细胞系注释，以及它们的规模、基因型信息和许可。它依据的是数据集页面和卡片（Q136）以及为 Tahoe-100M 推导的元数据计数（Q137）；没有下载任何数据文件。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

The two drug-transcriptome backbones differ in what they carry. LINCS L1000 holds 1,319,138 perturbational profiles [DS03] on about 70 cell lines, and its packaged reference collapses dose and time to 10 uM and 24 h [DS04]. Tahoe-100M adds 100,648,790 cells over 50 cell lines and 379 drugs at 3 doses, with per-line driver annotation, but its drug-target labels are model-proposed and 975 of 1,138 conditions are a single well (derived, Q137) [DS07]. Neither carries donor-level germline variation beyond cell-line identity [DS03, DS07].

> 两个药物转录组骨干数据集所含内容不同。LINCS L1000 有 1,319,138 个扰动谱 [DS03]，覆盖约 70 个细胞系，其打包参考把剂量和时间合并为 10 uM 和 24 h [DS04]。Tahoe-100M 加入了 50 个细胞系上的 100,648,790 个细胞和 379 种药物各 3 个剂量，并带有逐系驱动突变注释，但其药物靶点标签由模型提出，且 1,138 个条件中有 975 个只有一个孔（推导，Q137）[DS07]。两者都不含细胞系身份之外的供体层面种系变异 [DS03, DS07]。

In Tahoe-100M every line carries at least one annotated driver; TP53 is annotated in 33 of 50 lines, KRAS in 24, CDKN2A in 22 and BRAF in 9, and lung, bowel and pancreas lines number 15, 9 and 7. In the 12 lineage-matched mutant-versus-other contrasts computed, the smaller arm holds 1 to 6 lines (TP53 in lung: 12 versus 3; derived, Q137) [DS07].

> 在 Tahoe-100M 中，每个细胞系至少携带一个被注释的驱动突变；50 个系里 TP53 有 33 个被注释，KRAS 有 24 个，CDKN2A 有 22 个，BRAF 有 9 个，肺、肠和胰腺的系分别有 15、9 和 7 个。在已计算的 12 个按谱系匹配的突变株对其他株对比中，较小的一臂有 1 至 6 个系（肺中 TP53：12 对 3；推导，Q137）[DS07]。

Perturb-seq resources are mostly genetic and mostly single-background. A CRISPRi atlas covers 11,739 genes in one iPSC line [DS09]; genome-scale screens in K562 and RPE1 reached median knockdown of 85.5% and 91.6%, about 31% of expressed-gene knockdowns changed the transcriptome, and K562-versus-RPE1 phenotype similarity was low (median r 0.23) [DS19]; X-Atlas/Orion has eight million cells with median knockdown of 75.4% and 51.5% in two lines, the closest public proxy for graded, hypomorphic alleles, under a non-commercial share-alike licence [DS24]; and a CD4+ T-cell screen covers 22 million cells from four donors [DS08]. scPerturb harmonizes 44 public datasets and offers the E-distance with a test as a distribution-level distance [DS20].

> Perturb-seq 资源大多是遗传性的，且大多只有单一背景。一个 CRISPRi 图谱在一个 iPSC 系中覆盖 11,739 个基因 [DS09]；K562 和 RPE1 中的基因组规模筛选达到的敲低中位数为 85.5% 和 91.6%，约 31% 的表达基因敲低改变了转录组，而 K562 与 RPE1 之间的表型相似度很低（中位 r 为 0.23）[DS19]；X-Atlas/Orion 在两个系中有八百万个细胞，敲低中位数为 75.4% 和 51.5%，是梯度化、亚效等位基因最接近的公开代理，但采用非商业的相同方式共享许可 [DS24]；一个 CD4+ T 细胞筛选覆盖来自四名供体的 22 百万（2,200 万）个细胞 [DS08]。scPerturb 整合了 44 个公开数据集，并提供带检验的 E-distance 作为分布层面的距离 [DS20]。

Single-cell chemical and cytokine screens add dose and donor axes. sci-Plex covers 188 compounds at four doses in 3 cell lines (649,340 cells) [DS15]; the Parse PBMC atlas holds 9,697,974 cells from 12 donors treated with 90 cytokines, under CC BY-NC 4.0 [DS16]; and OP3 has 146 small molecules in PBMCs from 3 donors [DS17]. None of these records gives donor genotypes, so the T-cell and PBMC grids test donor effects, not genotype effects (UQ17).

> 单细胞化学和细胞因子筛选加入了剂量轴和供体轴。sci-Plex 在 3 个细胞系中覆盖 188 种化合物的四个剂量（649,340 个细胞）[DS15]；Parse PBMC 图谱有来自 12 名供体、经 90 种细胞因子处理的 9,697,974 个细胞，采用 CC BY-NC 4.0 许可 [DS16]；OP3 有 3 名供体 PBMC 中的 146 种小分子 [DS17]。这些记录都没有给出供体基因型，因此 T 细胞和 PBMC 网格检验的是供体效应而不是基因型效应（UQ17）。

For the germline track, the doxorubicin response-QTL data are an open Zenodo record (45 individuals, five concentrations, 447 response eQTLs, CC0) that lists expression and QTL tables but no genotype file [DS13]. The prediction targets of the DepMap, GDSC and PRISM compendia are viability and sensitivity, not transcriptomes [EV17].

> 对种系轨道，阿霉素响应 QTL 数据是一个公开的 Zenodo 记录（45 个个体、5 个浓度、447 个响应 eQTL，CC0），列出了表达和 QTL 表，但没有基因型文件 [DS13]。DepMap、GDSC 和 PRISM 汇编的预测目标是活力和敏感性，而不是转录组 [EV17]。

Time-resolved and closed-loop data are sparse. Metabolic labeling separates new from old RNA, which resolved brief versus sustained stimulation in about 55,000 mouse neurons [DS21] and gave measured state-transition supervision after a hypomethylating drug in about 16,000 HCT116 cells at 4 time points [DS22]. LabCompass publishes about 60 GB of protein-level closed-loop measurements [DS23], and no transcriptome-level closed-loop data were found (Q51).

> 时间分辨数据和闭环数据很稀少。代谢标记把新旧 RNA 分开，在约 55,000 个小鼠神经元中分辨出短暂与持续刺激 [DS21]，并在约 16,000 个 HCT116 细胞中、于 4 个时间点给出了去甲基化药物之后实测的状态转移监督 [DS22]。LabCompass 公布了约 60 GB 蛋白水平的闭环测量数据 [DS23]，没有找到转录组层面的闭环数据（Q51）。

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J12 (confidence: medium). Neither L1000 nor Tahoe-100M carries donor-level germline variation beyond cell-line identity, so they can support the somatic track but not, by themselves, the germline track [DS03, DS07].

> J12（置信度：中）。L1000 和 Tahoe-100M 都不含细胞系身份之外的供体层面种系变异，因此它们可以支持体细胞轨道，但单靠它们不能支持种系轨道 [DS03, DS07]。

J21 (confidence: low-medium). The donor-by-perturbation grids (the Parse PBMC atlas, OP3 and the Stack post-training data) are the largest testbeds for unseen-donor generalization and for the mediation ablation in a germline-like setting, but without genotypes they test donor effects, not genotype effects [DS16, DS17, MD42]. X-Atlas/Orion and the K562 and RPE1 screens give graded knockdown, the closest public proxy for hypomorphic alleles when no drug is present [DS24, DS19].

> J21（置信度：低中）。供体×扰动网格（Parse PBMC 图谱、OP3 和 Stack 后训练数据）是检验未见供体泛化和类种系情境下中介消融的最大试验场，但没有基因型时它们检验的是供体效应而不是基因型效应 [DS16, DS17, MD42]。X-Atlas/Orion 以及 K562 和 RPE1 筛选给出梯度化的敲低，是无药时亚效等位基因最接近的公开代理 [DS24, DS19]。

J29 (confidence: medium). Tahoe-100M supports the HA test only as small lineage-matched contrasts: every line carries a driver, so non-mutant lines are not wild type, and the drug-target labels are model-proposed, so power and drug-class strata must be treated accordingly [DS07].

> J29（置信度：中）。Tahoe-100M 只能以小规模的、按谱系匹配的对比来支持 HA 检验：每个细胞系都携带驱动突变，因此非突变系并非野生型，而药物靶点标签由模型提出，所以检验功效和药物类别分层必须据此对待 [DS07]。

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Use Tahoe-100M as the backbone of the somatic track and LINCS L1000 for breadth across cell lines and compounds, join both to cell-line mutation annotation through a stable identifier, and build the drug-class table from an independent curated source rather than from the model-proposed labels [DS07, DS03].

> 以 Tahoe-100M 为体细胞轨道的骨干，以 LINCS L1000 提供跨细胞系和化合物的广度，通过稳定的标识符把两者与细胞系突变注释连接，并从独立的整理来源而不是模型提出的标签建立药物类别表 [DS07, DS03]。

Define the lineage-matched contrasts from the counts above and fix the minimum number of lines per contrast by a power calculation, because the power of any genotype contrast depends on them.

> 根据上述计数定义按谱系匹配的对比，并通过效能计算确定每个对比所需的最少细胞系数，因为任何基因型对比的效能都取决于这些计数。

Treat composition effects as a first-class issue: a genotype that changes survival changes the mix of surviving cells, and the single-cell profiles retain proliferation and cell-cycle signals [DS06].

> 把组成效应作为头等问题处理：改变存活率的基因型会改变存活细胞的构成，而单细胞谱保留了增殖和细胞周期信号 [DS06]。

Check licences before use: Tahoe-100M and the Zenodo doxorubicin record are CC0 [DS07, DS13], whereas X-Atlas/Orion is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 [DS24] and the Parse atlas CC BY-NC 4.0 [DS16].

> 使用之前核查许可：Tahoe-100M 和 Zenodo 阿霉素记录为 CC0 [DS07, DS13]，而 X-Atlas/Orion 为 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 [DS24]，Parse 图谱为 CC BY-NC 4.0 [DS16]。

Check whether the donors of the Parse atlas and of OP3 can be linked to genotypes, calibrate gene-loss effect sizes on X-Atlas/Orion and the K562 and RPE1 screens, and use the E-distance of scPerturb as a distribution-level distance for the interaction score [DS16, DS17, DS24, DS19, DS20].

> 核查 Parse 图谱和 OP3 的供体能否与基因型关联，在 X-Atlas/Orion 以及 K562 和 RPE1 筛选上校准基因丢失的效应大小，并把 scPerturb 的 E-distance 用作交互评分的分布层面距离 [DS16, DS17, DS24, DS19, DS20]。
