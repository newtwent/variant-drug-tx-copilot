# 05 Perturbation-prediction models / 扰动预测模型

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document surveys the model families that predict transcriptional responses to perturbations and asks one question of each: how could inherited or somatic genotype enter? It covers chemistry-conditioned models, generative flow and diffusion models, the Arc State model, mechanism-informed networks, causal representation learning and simple baselines. Benchmarks and metrics are in document 07, and world-model claims are in document 06.

> 本文档综述预测对扰动的转录响应的模型家族，并对每一类提出一个问题：遗传或体细胞基因型如何进入模型？涵盖以化学为条件的模型、生成式流模型与扩散模型、Arc 的 State 模型、机制导向网络、因果表示学习以及简单基线。基准和指标在文档 07 中，世界模型的主张在文档 06 中。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

Chemistry-conditioned models predict responses to compounds that were not seen in training. chemCPA represents each drug with a fixed Morgan fingerprint derived from its SMILES string [MD01]. PRnet conditions on the compound's SMILES and an unperturbed expression profile, was trained on bulk L1000 and the single-cell sci-Plex3 data, and self-reports an average Pearson correlation of 0.8 on unseen compounds [MD02]. TranSiGen predicts the 978 landmark genes and then infers the rest of the transcriptome, and was evaluated with chemical-blind and cell-blind splits [MD03]. A third-party benchmark reports less favorable numbers on different metrics, so they are not directly comparable: for PRnet on a scaffold split, MAE 0.87 and Spearman 0.50, with TranSiGen similar [MD04].

> 以化学为条件的模型可预测训练中未见过的化合物的响应。chemCPA 用从 SMILES 字符串导出的固定 Morgan 指纹表示每种药物 [MD01]。PRnet 以化合物的 SMILES 和未扰动表达谱为条件，在 bulk L1000 和单细胞 sci-Plex3 数据上训练，自报在未见化合物上的平均 Pearson 相关为 0.8 [MD02]。TranSiGen 预测 978 个 landmark 基因，再推断转录组的其余部分，并用化合物盲和细胞系盲的划分进行评估 [MD03]。一项第三方基准在不同指标上报告了不那么有利的数字，因此二者不能直接比较：PRnet 在骨架划分上 MAE 为 0.87、Spearman 为 0.50，TranSiGen 与之相近 [MD04]。

Generative models treat the response as a shift between cell populations. CellFlow combines optimal-transport pairing of unperturbed and perturbed cells with conditional flow matching, aggregates sets of perturbations in a permutation-invariant way, and uses pretrained embeddings of biological entities for out-of-distribution prediction, often in PCA or VAE latent spaces [MD05]. Diffusion models for single-cell drug response [MD08], discrete diffusion with regulation reinforcement [MD24] and distributional flow matching [MD09] follow the same generative line.

> 生成式模型把响应视为细胞群体之间的偏移。CellFlow 把未扰动与已扰动细胞的最优传输配对和条件流匹配结合起来，以置换不变的方式聚合扰动集合，并使用生物实体的预训练嵌入做分布外预测，通常在 PCA 或 VAE 潜空间中进行 [MD05]。用于单细胞药物响应的扩散模型 [MD08]、带调控强化的离散扩散 [MD24] 以及分布式流匹配 [MD09] 沿着同一条生成式路线。

The Arc State model has two modules: a State Embedding model that represents individual cells and a State Transition model that predicts perturbation effects across cell populations [MD06]. Arc's launch statements report training on observational data from about 170 million cells and perturbational data from over 100 million cells across 70 cell lines, and a 50% gain in perturbation discrimination on Tahoe-100M; these are launch statements, not independent evaluation [MD07].

> Arc 的 State 模型有两个模块：表示单个细胞的 State Embedding 模型，以及预测细胞群体扰动效应的 State Transition 模型 [MD06]。Arc 的发布声明报告其在约 1.7 亿（170 million）个细胞的观测数据和 70 个细胞系中超过 1 亿（100 million）个细胞的扰动数据上训练，并在 Tahoe-100M 上扰动区分度提高 50%；这些是发布声明，不是独立评测 [MD07]。

Mechanism-informed networks put prior biology into the architecture. LEMBAS is a recurrent network on a signaling graph [MD12], and a phosphoproteomic extension can be queried with a perturbation such as drug-target inhibition without providing phosphoproteomic measurements [MD14]. P-NET builds a hierarchical network from 3,007 pathways [MD15], and a simulation study evaluates how such networks perform [MD16].

> 机制导向网络把先验生物学放入架构。LEMBAS 是建立在信号图上的循环网络 [MD12]，其磷酸化蛋白质组扩展可以用药物靶点抑制之类的扰动来查询，而无需提供磷酸化蛋白质组测量 [MD14]。P-NET 由 3,007 条通路构建层级网络 [MD15]，一项模拟研究评估了这类网络的表现 [MD16]。

Causal representation learning treats a perturbation as an intervention on latent variables. The sparse-mechanism-shift model assumes that an intervention changes only a few latent mechanisms, and its benefit shrinks when effects are small [MD17]. A factorized model splits the representation into covariate, treatment and interaction blocks and proves that the interaction block is identifiable component by component [MD18]. Other work reports identifiability for latent dynamical processes [MD19] and for soft interventions [MD20]; a talk abstract claims identifiability with unobserved causal variables under a generalized faithfulness condition, a tier-T3 source with no retrievable year [MD21].

> 因果表示学习把扰动视为对潜变量的干预。稀疏机制偏移模型假设干预只改变少数潜在机制，当效应较小时其收益会下降 [MD17]。一个因子化模型把表示分成协变量、处理和交互三块，并证明交互块可逐分量识别 [MD18]。其他工作报告了潜在动力学过程 [MD19] 和软干预 [MD20] 的可识别性；一份讲座摘要声称在广义忠实性条件下，即使因果变量未被观测也可识别，这是年份无法取得的 T3 级来源 [MD21]。

Simple models are competitive. OCOO-T denoises continuous expression profiles with a plain Transformer and no autoencoder [MD23], and a preprint title states that parameter-free representations outperform single-cell foundation models on downstream benchmarks, which is title-level information only [EV13]. The baseline critique is in document 07.

> 简单模型很有竞争力。OCOO-T 用普通 Transformer 直接对连续表达谱去噪，不使用自编码器 [MD23]，一篇预印本的标题称无参数表示在下游基准上优于单细胞基础模型，这只是标题级信息 [EV13]。基线批判见文档 07。

Current models take perturbation labels, cell type or donor identity as conditions, not inherited donor genotype. PertAdapt attaches a perturbation encoder to a pretrained single-cell model, and its inputs are gene edits [MD11]. pertTF lets embedding weights favor either genotype (perturbation) prediction or cell-type separation, and was transferred from stem-cell-derived data to primary human islets, where donor-level shifts in predicted perturbation states were detected [MD10]. Lingshu-Cell conditions on cell type or donor identity plus perturbation [WM10]. I did not find a model that takes inherited donor genotype as an input (queries Q12 and Q31).

> 当前的模型以扰动标签、细胞类型或供体身份为条件，而不是遗传性的供体基因型。PertAdapt 在预训练的单细胞模型上附加扰动编码器，其输入是基因编辑 [MD11]。pertTF 让嵌入权重偏向基因型（扰动）预测或细胞类型区分，并从干细胞来源的数据迁移到原代人胰岛，在那里检测到预测扰动状态的供体层面偏移 [MD10]。Lingshu-Cell 以细胞类型或供体身份加扰动为条件 [WM10]。我没有找到以遗传性供体基因型为输入的模型（查询 Q12 和 Q31）。

Models with biological priors and basal-state encoders are the current reference. TxPert combines a basal-state encoder with a perturbation encoder over several knowledge graphs (STRING, GO and two Recursion-derived maps) in a graph transformer, and its authors state that predictions for unseen single perturbations approach split-half experimental reproducibility, with later work contesting the lead under the Systema protocol [MD37]. GEARS uses a gene–gene knowledge graph to predict single and multigene perturbations, including combinations containing genes never perturbed [MD41]. PrePR-CT uses cell-type-specific co-expression networks as an inductive bias in the small-data regime [MD45].

> 带生物学先验和基础状态编码器的模型是当前的参照。TxPert 把基础状态编码器与在多个知识图谱（STRING、GO 和 Recursion 衍生的两个图）上运行的图 Transformer 的扰动编码器结合起来，其作者称对未见单扰动的预测接近半数据重复实验的可重复性，后续工作在 Systema 协议下对这一领先地位提出异议 [MD37]。GEARS 用基因–基因知识图谱预测单基因和多基因扰动，包括含有从未被扰动过的基因的组合 [MD41]。PrePR-CT 在小数据情形下把细胞类型特异的共表达网络用作归纳偏置 [MD45]。

Covariate-recombination and disentangling models give the closest templates for a genotype covariate. CPA decomposes data into independent embeddings for cell type, perturbation and covariates that can be recombined for unseen combinations, with dose–response curves [MD40, MD58]. The Large Perturbation Model keeps perturbation, readout and context as separate conditioning dimensions so that it can train on experiments that do not fully overlap [MD36], and scLAMBDA separates basal from perturbation-salient representations [MD43]. A drug model with two branches for the state before and after perturbation and with dose and time tokens reports transfer from preclinical screens to patients, with an improvement of up to 15.04% (the authors' figure) [MD44].

> 协变量重组和解耦模型为基因型协变量提供了最接近的模板。CPA 把数据分解为细胞类型、扰动和协变量的相互独立嵌入，这些嵌入可重新组合以预测未见组合，并给出剂量–响应曲线 [MD40, MD58]。大型扰动模型（Large Perturbation Model）把扰动、读出和背景作为相互独立的条件维度，因此可以在不完全重叠的实验上训练 [MD36]，scLAMBDA 把基础表示与扰动显著表示分开 [MD43]。一个药物模型用两个分支分别处理扰动前后的状态，并带有剂量和时间标记，报告了从临床前筛选向患者的迁移，最多提高 15.04%（作者给出的数字）[MD44]。

Variants and donors can be represented in five ways, and none takes inherited genotype as input in the retrieved sources. Additive covariate embeddings are recombined for unseen combinations [MD40]; a perturbation–readout–context factorization is trained on non-overlapping experiments [MD36]; in-context adaptation uses unlabeled cells of another donor or condition, with a donor_id split as the only documented donor handling [MD42, MD65]; a donor-level embedding from sets of cells is transformed by a cytokine-conditioned operator [MD60]; and a variant's amino-acid-sequence embedding stands for the perturbation, applied to all point mutations of GATA1 [MD39]. CellFlow's documentation tutorial is reported to split the control distribution by donor so that one donor's controls are never mapped onto another donor's treated cells [MD61].

> 变异和供体可以用五种方式表示，而在检索到的来源中没有一种以遗传性基因型为输入。加性协变量嵌入被重新组合以预测未见组合 [MD40]；在不重叠的实验上训练“扰动-读出-背景”分解 [MD36]；上下文适应使用另一个供体或条件的无标签细胞，文档中唯一的供体处理方式是 donor_id 拆分 [MD42, MD65]；由细胞集合得到的供体层面嵌入再由细胞因子条件算子变换 [MD60]；以及用变异的氨基酸序列嵌入代表扰动，应用于 GATA1 的所有点突变 [MD39]。据报道，CellFlow 的文档教程按供体拆分对照分布，使某个供体的对照永远不会被映射到另一个供体的处理细胞上 [MD61]。

Cell-number changes and trajectories are handled by unbalanced and trajectory-aware models. U-Pert models both transcriptomic state transitions and cell-number changes from unpaired snapshots and offers inverse design [MD38], PerturbRx trains a drug- and dose-conditioned transition predictor on cell populations and applies it to patient profiles [MD46], and PerturbGen propagates an early perturbation along trajectories [MD59]. MolGene-E generates small molecules from expression profiles, which runs opposite to perturbation prediction [MD64].

> 细胞数量变化和轨迹由非平衡和轨迹感知模型处理。U-Pert 从非配对快照同时建模转录组状态转移和细胞数量变化，并提供逆向设计 [MD38]，PerturbRx 在细胞群体上训练以药物和剂量为条件的转移预测器并把它应用于患者谱 [MD46]，PerturbGen 沿轨迹传播早期扰动 [MD59]。MolGene-E 由表达谱生成小分子，其方向与扰动预测相反 [MD64]。

Mechanistic and visible-network models encode genotype through structure. CellBox fits a 99-component ODE with interpretable interaction parameters to drug-perturbation data [MD50]. A pan-cancer pathway model with more than 1,200 species is individualized per cell line through expression-driven synthesis rates and mutated variants [MD51], and drug combinations are optimized on it [MD52]. Logic models fit cell-line-specific and patient-specific parameters to a shared network [MD55, MD56]. DrugCell maps neurons to a hierarchy of cellular components and learns which subsystems contribute to response from the mutational profile [MD53], and in the yeast model DCell about 80% of the importance sits in 21% of subsystems [MD54].

> 机理模型和可见网络模型通过结构来编码基因型。CellBox 把一个 99 组分、带可解释相互作用参数的 ODE 拟合到药物扰动数据上 [MD50]。一个含 1,200 多个物种的泛癌通路模型通过由表达驱动的合成速率和突变变体来个体化每个细胞系 [MD51]，并在其上优化药物组合 [MD52]。逻辑模型把细胞系特异和患者特异的参数拟合到共享网络上 [MD55, MD56]。DrugCell 把神经元对应到细胞组分的层级，并根据突变谱学习哪些子系统对响应有贡献 [MD53]，而在酵母模型 DCell 中约 80% 的重要性集中在 21% 的子系统中 [MD54]。

Scale and implementation. Tahoe-x1 is a perturbation-trained foundation model family with up to about 3 billion parameters, pretrained on 266 million profiles [MD47], and X-Cell is a 4.9-billion-parameter diffusion model announced by its company and not independently verified [MD57]. The repositories read in this iteration are CPA (BSD-3-Clause) [MD58], PerturbGen (MIT) [MD59], CellFlow (MIT) [MD63], ReconEval (MIT) [MD66] and IterPert (Apache-2.0) [MD49]; State and Stack have code under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and weights under non-commercial licences [MD62, MD65].

> 规模与实现。Tahoe-x1 是以扰动数据训练的基础模型家族，最大约 30 亿（3 billion）参数，在 266 million（2.66 亿）个谱上预训练 [MD47]；X-Cell 是由其公司公布的 4.9 billion（49 亿）参数扩散模型，未经独立验证 [MD57]。本轮读过的仓库有 CPA（BSD-3-Clause）[MD58]、PerturbGen（MIT）[MD59]、CellFlow（MIT）[MD63]、ReconEval（MIT）[MD66] 和 IterPert（Apache-2.0）[MD49]；State 和 Stack 的代码采用 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0，权重采用非商业许可 [MD62, MD65]。

Two resources test the surrounding machinery. IterPert retrains after each wet-lab round and picks the next batch of perturbations, framed as active learning under budget [MD48, MD49], and ReconEval tests whether predicted latent shifts decode into correct expression [MD66]. CausalBench found that causal-network inference from perturbation data did not benefit from interventional data [MD67].

> 两项资源检验周边机制。IterPert 在每轮湿实验之后重新训练并挑选下一批扰动，被表述为预算受限下的主动学习 [MD48, MD49]；ReconEval 检验预测的潜空间偏移能否解码为正确的表达 [MD66]。CausalBench 发现，从扰动数据推断因果网络并未从干预数据中获益 [MD67]。

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J4 (raised; confidence: low-medium; untested for transcriptome-wide drug responses). A variant may act as a sparse intervention on the transition operator rather than as a global shift of the state. Several independent anchors agree: about 1% of allelic variants in one reporter assay interact with a drug [GD50], 21% of subsystems carry about 80% of the importance in a yeast genotype model [MD54], perturbation-specific signal is sparse relative to invariant structure [MD22], and in TP53-mutant lines a programme is switched off rather than rewritten [GD45]. This also agrees with reQTLs being sparse across the transcriptome and with the sparse-mechanism-shift literature [MD17, MD18], but the benefit of the sparsity assumption shrinks when effects are small [MD17], and no transcriptome-wide drug-response test exists.

> J4（上调；置信度：低中；对全转录组药物响应尚未检验）。变异作用于转移算子的方式可能是稀疏干预，而不是对状态的全局偏移。若干独立的锚点相互一致：在一项报告基因实验中约 1% 的等位基因变异与药物发生交互 [GD50]，在酵母基因型模型中 21% 的子系统承载约 80% 的重要性 [MD54]，相对不变结构而言扰动特异信号是稀疏的 [MD22]，在 TP53 突变系中是一个程序被关闭而不是被重写 [GD45]。这也与 reQTL 在转录组中稀疏、以及稀疏机制偏移文献一致 [MD17, MD18]，但当效应较小时稀疏假设的收益会下降 [MD17]，而且不存在全转录组的药物响应检验。

J15 (confidence: low). The covariate–treatment–interaction split of the factorized model maps naturally onto genotype, drug and genotype-by-drug interaction, so it is a candidate formalism for the interaction term, but its identifiability results rest on assumptions that real data may not meet [MD18, MD17, MD19].

> J15（置信度：低）。因子化模型的协变量-处理-交互划分可以自然地对应到基因型、药物和基因型×药物交互，因此它是处理交互项的候选形式化框架，但其可识别性结果依赖于真实数据可能不满足的假设 [MD18, MD17, MD19]。

J23 (confidence: medium). The most direct ways to add genotype are a covariate group in the condition encoder with a genotype-matched source population (CPA- or CellFlow-style) [MD40, MD63, MD61], a donor-level embedding on which a perturbation operator acts [MD60], and in-context adaptation from unlabeled cells [MD42]. A genotype-matched source is a design choice that the ablation of hypothesis HA tests directly (document 08).

> J23（置信度：中）。加入基因型最直接的方式是：在条件编码器中加一个协变量组并配以与基因型匹配的源群体（CPA 或 CellFlow 式）[MD40, MD63, MD61]，由扰动算子作用的供体层面嵌入 [MD60]，以及依据无标签细胞做上下文适应 [MD42]。与基因型匹配的源是一个设计选择，假设 HA 的消融直接检验它（文档 08）。

J24 (confidence: medium). Reuse is limited by licences and by evidence. Permissively licensed code (CPA, PerturbGen, CellFlow, ReconEval and IterPert) can serve as a base, whereas the State and Stack weights are non-commercial [MD58, MD59, MD63, MD66, MD49, MD62, MD65]. The retrieved descriptions give few independent performance numbers, so the baseline ladder remains the arbiter [MD37, MD57].

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Build a baseline ladder with matched capacity and identical splits: the mean response, a drug-wise linear model on the same inputs, and a one-step conditional generative model of the CellFlow or State type. Any proposed architecture must beat every rung on interaction-specific metrics [EV01, MD05, MD06].

> 建立容量匹配、划分相同的基线阶梯：均值响应、在相同输入上的逐药物线性模型，以及 CellFlow 或 State 类型的单步条件生成模型。任何提出的架构都必须在交互项专用指标上击败每一级 [EV01, MD05, MD06]。

Use chemistry encoders only if generalization to unseen compounds matters. In the somatic track with a fixed drug set, the drug can be a categorical label [MD01, MD02].

> 只有当对未见化合物的泛化重要时才使用化学编码器。在药物集合固定的体细胞轨道中，药物可以是类别标签 [MD01, MD02]。

Treat every self-reported performance number as unverified until it is reproduced, and prefer the third-party benchmark numbers where both exist [MD02, MD04].

> 在复现之前，把每个自报的性能数字都视为未经核验，并且在两者都存在时优先采用第三方基准的数字 [MD02, MD04]。

Before relying on a sparse-shift assumption, measure the effect sizes of the genotype contrast in the data at hand [MD17].

> 在依赖稀疏偏移假设之前，先在手头数据中测量基因型对比的效应大小 [MD17]。

Add a genotype covariate group to a CPA- or CellFlow-style baseline and keep the source population genotype-matched, then remove the covariate while keeping the matched source, which is the HA ablation of document 08 [MD40, MD63].

> 给 CPA 或 CellFlow 式的基线加入基因型协变量组，并保持源群体与基因型匹配，然后在保持匹配源的同时去掉该协变量，这就是文档 08 中的 HA 消融 [MD40, MD63]。

Try the donor-embedding route and the in-context route as alternative carriers of genotype in the germline-like setting [MD60, MD42], and flag the non-commercial terms of State and Stack before any reuse [MD62, MD65].

> 在类种系情形下，尝试供体嵌入途径和上下文途径作为基因型的替代载体 [MD60, MD42]，并在任何复用之前标注 State 和 Stack 的非商业条款 [MD62, MD65]。
