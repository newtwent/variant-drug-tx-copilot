# 05 Perturbation-prediction models / 扰动预测模型

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document surveys the model families that predict transcriptional responses to perturbations and asks one question of each: how could inherited or somatic genotype enter? Benchmarks and metrics are in document 07, and world-model claims are in document 06.

> 本文档综述预测扰动转录响应的模型家族，并对每一类提出同一个问题：遗传性或体细胞基因型如何进入？基准和指标在文档 07 中，世界模型主张在文档 06 中。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

Chemistry-conditioned models predict responses to unseen compounds with modest, hard-to-compare gains. chemCPA adds cell-line and dose-scaled drug shifts, encoded by frozen RDKit features, to an adversarially covariate-invariant basal state [MD01]; PRnet conditions on a dosage-scaled fingerprint and an unperturbed profile and self-reports an average Pearson of 0.8 on per-compound mean log fold changes of unseen L1000 compounds [MD02]; TranSiGen predicts the 978-gene delta from a basal-profile latent, with held-out-line accuracy rising across 10, 50 and 150 training lines [MD03]. On a scaffold-held-out split five models span only 0.46 to 0.52 Spearman and R2 0.20 to 0.27, a spread any genotype term must be judged against [MD04]. A cheap dose-axis baseline interpolates one latent response direction log-linearly in dose [MD70].

> 化学条件模型对未见化合物的响应预测，增益不大且难以比较。chemCPA 把细胞系和按剂量缩放的药物偏移（由冻结的 RDKit 特征编码）加到与协变量无关的对抗性基础状态上 [MD01]；PRnet 以按剂量缩放的指纹和未扰动谱为条件，自报在未见 L1000 化合物的逐化合物平均对数倍数变化上平均 Pearson 为 0.8 [MD02]；TranSiGen 由基础谱潜变量预测 978 基因的 delta，留出细胞系的准确度随训练细胞系数 10、50 和 150 上升 [MD03]。在按骨架留出的划分上，五个模型只覆盖 0.46 至 0.52 的 Spearman 和 0.20 至 0.27 的 R2，任何基因型项都要对照这一离散范围来评判 [MD04]。一个廉价的剂量轴基线是把一个潜在响应方向按剂量做对数线性插值 [MD70]。

Generative models treat the response as a shift between cell populations. CellFlow uses optimal-transport pairing with conditional flow matching and pretrained embeddings, with a donor-specific PBMC cytokine example (preprint results unread) [MD05]; ablations of a flow model in gene space favored gene space over a PCA latent, a U-Net over an MLP and independent over optimal-transport coupling, and mean-based and distribution-based metrics ranked models differently [MD73]. Diffusion and flow variants add a plate-matched baseline state with classifier-free guidance (Spearman 0.49 versus 0.36 on unseen Tahoe-100M drugs, self-reported) [MD08], a gene-generation order set by a regulatory prior that helps while its reverse hurts [MD24], a distribution-level loss [MD09] and a population-level objective for hidden donor or batch variation [MD69]. State uses set-level transitions over cell embeddings trained on nearly 170 million observational cells, with a launch claim of 50 percent better effect discrimination on Tahoe-100M (self-reported) [MD06, MD07].

> 生成模型把响应视为细胞群体之间的偏移。CellFlow 使用最优传输配对加条件流匹配以及预训练嵌入，并有按供体区分的 PBMC 细胞因子示例（预印本结果未读）[MD05]；对基因空间流模型的消融显示，基因空间优于 PCA 潜空间，U-Net 优于 MLP，独立耦合优于最优传输耦合，并且基于均值的指标与基于分布的指标对模型的排序不同 [MD73]。扩散和流的变体加入了带无分类器引导的同板基线状态（在未见的 Tahoe-100M 药物上 Spearman 为 0.49 对 0.36，自报）[MD08]、由调控先验设定的基因生成顺序（先验有帮助，反向则有害）[MD24]、分布层面的损失 [MD09]，以及针对隐藏的供体或批次变异的群体层面目标 [MD69]。State 使用对细胞嵌入的集合层面转移，在近 170 百万（1.7 亿）个观测细胞上训练，发布时声称在 Tahoe-100M 上效应区分度提高 50%（自报）[MD06, MD07]。

No model in the sources takes inherited donor genotype as input; donors and variants are represented in six ways. Additive covariate embeddings are recombined for unseen combinations, although a random-cell baseline matches CPA on R2 [MD40]; a perturbation–readout–context factorization trains on non-overlapping experiments but treats a genotype symbol as out of vocabulary for new donors [MD36]; in-context adaptation uses unlabeled cells of another donor, with a donor_id split as the only documented donor handling [MD42, MD65]; a donor-level embedding from sets of cells is shifted by a cytokine-conditioned operator and cut unseen-donor RMSE by 42.5% against a linear baseline (self-reported) [MD60]; a variant's amino-acid-sequence embedding stands for the perturbation, yet a nearest-neighbour baseline beat it for TP53 and KRAS [MD39]; and DNA sequence at the perturbed locus specifies a perturbation, with regulatory variants untested [MD71]. Controls matter: without control cells from an unseen line a mean baseline beat a graph model on most Tahoe-100M lines, so a new genotype needs baseline cells [MD45], and the CellFlow tutorial splits controls by donor [MD61]. Graded gene loss maps nonlinearly and gene-specifically to the response, with 395 of 488 fitted genes buffered [MD68].

> 来源中没有任何模型把遗传性供体基因型作为输入；供体和变异以六种方式表示。加性协变量嵌入可为未见组合重新组合，尽管随机细胞基线在 R2 上与 CPA 相当 [MD40]；扰动-读出-背景分解可在不完全重叠的实验上训练，但对新供体而言基因型符号属于词表之外 [MD36]；上下文内适应使用另一名供体的未标注细胞，唯一记录的供体处理是 donor_id 划分 [MD42, MD65]；由细胞集合得到的供体层面嵌入被细胞因子条件的算子推动，相对线性基线把未见供体的 RMSE 降低了 42.5%（自报）[MD60]；变异的氨基酸序列嵌入代表该扰动，但最近邻基线在 TP53 和 KRAS 上胜过它 [MD39]；被扰动位点的 DNA 序列可以指定一个扰动，调控变异则未经检验 [MD71]。对照很重要：没有未见系的对照细胞时，均值基线在大多数 Tahoe-100M 细胞系上胜过图模型，所以新基因型需要基线细胞 [MD45]，而 CellFlow 教程按供体拆分对照 [MD61]。梯度化的基因丢失以非线性、基因特异的方式映射到响应，488 个被拟合基因中有 395 个被缓冲 [MD68]。

Mechanistic and visible-network models encode genotype through structure. LEMBAS puts weights only on known interactions, and a phosphoproteomic extension specifies an unseen drug by its target node (R 0.79 for one inhibitor and no gain for another, self-reported) [MD13, MD14]; DrugCell maps the mutation status of 3,008 genes through 2,086 subsystems, with a pooled rho of 0.80 against a per-drug median of 0.37 [MD53]; P-NET favors small samples, and germline plus somatic variants gave no accuracy gain over somatic alone, only a better BRCA2 rank [MD15, MD16]; a pan-cancer pathway model of 1,228 species is individualized by expression and gain-of-function mutations but assumes static transcription [MD52]; and phosphoprotein logic-model parameters tracked sensitivity for 9 drugs with no genomic biomarker (abstract level) [MD55].

> 机制导向和可见网络模型通过结构编码基因型。LEMBAS 只在已知相互作用上设置权重，其磷酸化蛋白质组扩展用靶点节点来指定未见药物（一种抑制剂 R 为 0.79，另一种没有增益，自报）[MD13, MD14]；DrugCell 把 3,008 个基因的突变状态经由 2,086 个子系统映射，合并 rho 为 0.80，而逐药物中位数为 0.37 [MD53]；P-NET 在小样本下占优，而种系加体细胞变异相对只用体细胞并没有带来准确度增益，只是改善了 BRCA2 的排名 [MD15, MD16]；一个含 1,228 个物种的泛癌通路模型通过表达和功能获得突变做个体化，但假定转录是静态的 [MD52]；磷酸化蛋白逻辑模型的参数对 9 种没有基因组生物标志物的药物追踪了敏感性（摘要层面）[MD55]。

Causal representation learning treats a perturbation as an intervention on latent variables. The sparse-mechanism-shift model gains less when effects are small [MD17]; a factorized model splits covariate, treatment and interaction blocks and proves the interaction block identifiable component by component, yet leads CPA by at most 0.03 R2 [MD18]; identifiability results for latent dynamics and soft interventions rest on assumptions that real data may not meet [MD19, MD20]; and a CRISPR-screen model works best with 85 invariant and 20 responsive latents [MD22].

> 因果表示学习把扰动视为对潜变量的干预。稀疏机制偏移模型在效应较小时收益变小 [MD17]；一个因子化模型把表示分为协变量、处理和交互块，并证明交互块可逐分量识别，但相对 CPA 的领先至多只有 0.03 的 R2 [MD18]；潜在动力学和软干预的可识别性结果依赖于真实数据未必满足的假设 [MD19, MD20]；一个 CRISPR 筛选模型在 85 个不变潜变量加 20 个响应潜变量时效果最好 [MD22]。

Cell-number changes, trajectories and design are handled by further models. U-Pert models state transitions and cell-number change from unpaired snapshots and offers inverse design [MD38]; PerturbRx applies a drug- and dose-conditioned transition predictor to patient profiles, with a gain over static features below one standard deviation on one cohort [MD46]; PerturbGen propagates an early perturbation along trajectories [MD59]. Reinforcement fine-tuning with metric rewards showed reward hacking when a single metric was optimized [MD74], and drug–gene edges were the most useful knowledge for unprofiled drugs [MD72].

> 细胞数量变化、轨迹和设计由更多模型处理。U-Pert 由未配对快照建模状态转移和细胞数量变化，并提供逆向设计 [MD38]；PerturbRx 把药物和剂量条件的转移预测器应用于患者谱，在一个队列上对静态特征的增益低于一个标准差 [MD46]；PerturbGen 沿轨迹传播早期扰动 [MD59]。以指标作为奖励的强化微调在只优化单一指标时出现奖励投机 [MD74]，而药物-基因边是未分析药物最有用的知识 [MD72]。

Scale does not substitute for diversity. Tahoe-x1 is pretrained on 266 million profiles with a drug token, up to 3 billion parameters [MD47], and for X-Cell, a company report, fold-change correlation plateaus near 0.31 above 1.6 billion parameters, with perturbation-context diversity named as the bottleneck [MD57]. IterPert retrains after each wet-lab round and picks the next batch of perturbations [MD48, MD49], ReconEval tests whether predicted latent shifts decode into correct expression [MD66], and CausalBench found that interventional data helped network inference only for methods that exploit them [MD67].

> 规模不能替代多样性。Tahoe-x1 在 266 百万（2.66 亿）个带药物标记的谱上预训练，最多 3 十亿（30 亿）参数 [MD47]；对公司报告 X-Cell 而言，超过 1.6 十亿（16 亿）参数后倍数变化相关性在 0.31 附近趋于平台，并把扰动-背景多样性列为瓶颈 [MD57]。IterPert 在每轮湿实验之后重新训练并挑选下一批扰动 [MD48, MD49]，ReconEval 检验预测的潜在偏移能否解码为正确的表达 [MD66]，CausalBench 发现干预数据只对善于利用它们的方法有帮助 [MD67]。

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J4 (confidence: low-medium; untested for transcriptome-wide drug responses). A variant may act as a sparse intervention on the transition operator rather than as a global shift of the state. Anchors agree but none is a transcriptome-wide drug-response test: 6 of 15,927 tested variants interacted with caffeine in a reporter assay [GD50], a CRISPR-screen model works best with 85 invariant and 20 responsive latents [MD22], and in TP53-mutant lines a programme is switched off rather than rewritten [GD45]. The benefit of the sparsity assumption shrinks when effects are small [MD17].

> J4（置信度：低中；对全转录组药物响应未经检验）。变异可能作为对转移算子的稀疏干预而起作用，而不是对状态的全局偏移。若干锚点一致，但没有一个是全转录组的药物响应检验：在报告基因实验中，15,927 个被检测变异里有 6 个与咖啡因发生交互 [GD50]；一个 CRISPR 筛选模型在 85 个不变潜变量加 20 个响应潜变量时效果最好 [MD22]；在 TP53 突变系中是一个程序被关闭而不是被重写 [GD45]。效应较小时，稀疏性假设的收益变小 [MD17]。

J15 (confidence: low). The covariate–treatment–interaction split of the factorized model maps naturally onto genotype, drug and genotype-by-drug interaction, so it is a candidate formalism for the interaction term, but its identifiability results rest on assumptions that real data may not meet [MD18, MD17, MD19].

> J15（置信度：低）。因子化模型的协变量-处理-交互划分自然对应基因型、药物和基因型×药物交互，因此它是交互项的候选形式化，但其可识别性结果依赖于真实数据未必满足的假设 [MD18, MD17, MD19]。

J23 (confidence: medium). The most direct ways to add genotype are a covariate group in the condition encoder with a genotype-matched source population (CPA- or CellFlow-style) [MD40, MD61], a donor-level embedding on which a perturbation operator acts [MD60] and in-context adaptation from unlabeled cells [MD42]; hypomorphic or copy-number inputs should be graded covariates, not binary flags [MD68]. A genotype-matched source is a design choice that the ablation of hypothesis HA tests directly (document 08).

> J23（置信度：中）。加入基因型最直接的方式是：在条件编码器中加入协变量组并配以与基因型匹配的源群体（CPA 或 CellFlow 式）[MD40, MD61]；用供体层面嵌入并让扰动算子作用其上 [MD60]；以及由未标注细胞做上下文内适应 [MD42]；亚效等位基因或拷贝数输入应当是梯度化的协变量，而不是二元标记 [MD68]。与基因型匹配的源群体是一项设计选择，假设 HA 的消融可直接检验它（文档 08）。

J24 (confidence: medium). Reuse is limited by licences and by evidence. Permissively licensed code (PerturbGen, IterPert) can serve as a base, whereas the State and Stack weights are non-commercial and the CPA and cellflow licence files conflict with their project files [MD59, MD49, MD62, MD65, MD58, MD63]. Few independent performance numbers exist, so the baseline ladder remains the arbiter [MD37, MD57].

> J24（置信度：中）。复用受许可和证据的限制。采用宽松许可的代码（PerturbGen、IterPert）可作为基础，而 State 和 Stack 的权重为非商业，CPA 和 cellflow 的许可文件与其项目文件相互冲突 [MD59, MD49, MD62, MD65, MD58, MD63]。独立的性能数字很少，所以基线阶梯仍是裁判 [MD37, MD57]。

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Build a baseline ladder with matched capacity and identical splits: the mean response, a drug-wise linear model on the same inputs, and a one-step conditional generative model of the CellFlow or State type. Any proposed architecture must beat every rung on interaction-specific metrics [EV01, MD05, MD06].

> 构建容量匹配、划分一致的基线阶梯：均值响应、基于相同输入的逐药物线性模型，以及 CellFlow 或 State 类型的单步条件生成模型。任何提出的架构都必须在交互项专用指标上击败每一级 [EV01, MD05, MD06]。

Use chemistry encoders only if generalization to unseen compounds matters; in the somatic track with a fixed drug set, the drug can be a categorical label [MD01, MD02]. Treat every self-reported performance number as unverified until it is reproduced [MD02, MD04].

> 只有在对未见化合物的泛化重要时才使用化学编码器；在药物集合固定的体细胞轨道中，药物可以是类别标签 [MD01, MD02]。在复现之前，把每个自报的性能数字都视为未经核验 [MD02, MD04]。

Add a genotype covariate group to a CPA- or CellFlow-style baseline and keep the source population genotype-matched, then remove the covariate while keeping the matched source, which is the HA ablation of document 08 [MD40, MD61]; try the donor-embedding and in-context routes as alternative carriers of genotype in the germline-like setting [MD60, MD42].

> 给 CPA 或 CellFlow 式基线加入基因型协变量组并保持源群体与基因型匹配，然后在保持匹配源的同时去掉该协变量，这就是文档 08 的 HA 消融 [MD40, MD61]；在类种系情境中尝试供体嵌入和上下文内两条路线作为基因型的替代载体 [MD60, MD42]。

Before relying on a sparse-shift assumption, measure the effect sizes of the genotype contrast in the data at hand [MD17], and flag the non-commercial terms of State and Stack before any reuse [MD62, MD65].

> 在依赖稀疏偏移假设之前，先测量手头数据中基因型对比的效应大小 [MD17]，并在任何复用之前标注 State 和 Stack 的非商业条款 [MD62, MD65]。

## 7. Evidence gaps / 证据缺口

No model that takes inherited donor genotype as input was found (Q12, Q31, Q76). The critique of chemCPA specifically was thin (Q07), and the details of the State paper beyond launch statements and an abstract were not read [MD06, MD07]. No independent head-to-head comparison of generative models on genotype dependence was found.

> 没有找到把遗传性供体基因型作为输入的模型（Q12、Q31、Q76）。专门针对 chemCPA 的批评很少（Q07），State 论文在发布声明和摘要之外的细节没有读到 [MD06, MD07]。没有找到针对基因型依赖性的、独立的生成模型直接比较。
