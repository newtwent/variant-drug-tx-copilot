# 06 World models and virtual cells / 世界模型与虚拟细胞

## 1. Scope / 范围

This document collects what the sources say about world models in general and about "world models" of the cell: definitions, architectures, claims and critiques. Its purpose is to turn the label into testable requirements, because the end goal of the project is a transcriptome predictor built on a world-model architecture. It does not evaluate any model empirically.

> 本文档汇集来源关于一般世界模型和细胞“世界模型”的说法：定义、架构、主张和批评。其目的是把这一标签转化为可检验的要求，因为本项目的最终目标是建立在世界模型架构上的转录组预测器。它不对任何模型做实证评估。

## 2. Facts and evidence / 事实与证据

Definitions converge on a persistent latent state with an action-conditioned transition. Dreamer learns a representation, transition and reward model and plans on imagined latent rollouts [WM01]; for cells, a review requires persistent state, intervention-driven evolution, counterfactual reasoning and long-horizon planning [WM16], and a formal form is an encoder, a transition z' = F(z, a) and a decoder, with multi-step stability and cross-modal consistency as requirements [WM17]. Surveys add that observational accuracy cannot guarantee counterfactual validity because latent dynamics are non-identifiable [WM02], that deterministic transitions invite planner exploitation [WM03] and that generation metrics alone do not reflect predictive rationality [WM04]; a critique holds that without an updatable state and a transition operator the claim is representational, not dynamical [WM21].

> 各种定义都收敛到带有动作条件转移的持久潜在状态。Dreamer 学习表示模型、转移模型和奖励模型，并在想象的潜在展开上做规划 [WM01]；对细胞而言，一篇综述要求持久状态、干预驱动的演化、反事实推理和长程规划 [WM16]，而一种形式化表述是编码器、转移 z' = F(z, a) 和解码器，并要求多步稳定性和跨模态一致性 [WM17]。综述还指出，由于潜在动力学不可识别，观测准确度不能保证反事实有效性 [WM02]，确定性转移会招致规划器的利用 [WM03]，仅靠生成指标并不能反映预测的合理性 [WM04]；一项批评认为，没有可更新的状态和转移算子，这一主张就只是表征性的，而不是动力学的 [WM21]。

Models that call themselves world models of the cell are preprints or repositories, none independently verified. AlphaCell models perturbations as flow-matching vector fields in a genome-wide latent [WM06]; Lingshu-Cell is a masked discrete diffusion model with cell-type or donor and perturbation tokens, whose PBMC test holds out cytokines within donors, so unseen-donor generalization is not shown [WM10]; Chreode's one-step residual transition helped only inside GEARS on one dataset (12.4% lower DE20 MSE, self-reported) [WM20]; VCLMU moves sparse latent mechanism units with small margins over a perturbed-mean baseline [WM19]; and VCWorld replaces regression with LLM reasoning over retrieved cases, with an F1 gap that is weak evidence because baseline outputs were binarized [WM08]. AROMA contributes evaluation protocols to copy: an unseen-cell-line holdout and test-gene stratification by literature popularity [WM09].

> 自称细胞世界模型的模型都是预印本或仓库，没有一个得到独立核验。AlphaCell 把扰动建模为基因组规模潜空间中的流匹配向量场 [WM06]；Lingshu-Cell 是带细胞类型或供体加扰动标记的掩码离散扩散模型，其 PBMC 检验是在供体内部留出细胞因子，所以没有展示未见供体的泛化 [WM10]；Chreode 的单步残差转移只在 GEARS 内部、在一个数据集上有帮助（DE20 的 MSE 降低 12.4%，自报）[WM20]；VCLMU 推动稀疏的潜在机制单元，相对扰动后均值基线的优势很小 [WM19]；VCWorld 用对检索案例的 LLM 推理取代回归，其 F1 差距只是弱证据，因为基线输出经过二值化 [WM08]。AROMA 贡献了值得照搬的评测方案：未见细胞系留出，以及按文献热度对测试基因分层 [WM09]。

JEPA-style latent prediction has reached transcriptomics. GeneJepa predicts latent representations of masked gene sets and is trained on Tahoe-100M (abstract level) [WM11]; Cell-JEPA raised Norman Pearson from 0.631 to 0.787 while delta Pearson fell from 0.103 to 0.059, a warning that state fidelity can trade against effect-size accuracy [WM12]; BioM-JEPA checks composition by additivity in embedding space, with a weak random-pair null [WM13]; and a DNA-model variant improves regulatory tasks, but its zero-shot reference-versus-variant similarity is weak (AUROC 0.503 to 0.574) [WM14].

> JEPA 式的潜在预测已进入转录组学。GeneJepa 预测被掩码基因集的潜在表示，并在 Tahoe-100M 上训练（摘要层面）[WM11]；Cell-JEPA 把 Norman 的 Pearson 从 0.631 提高到 0.787，同时 delta Pearson 从 0.103 降到 0.059，这警示状态保真度可能与效应大小准确度相互取舍 [WM12]；BioM-JEPA 通过嵌入空间中的加性来检验组合，其零假设只是随机配对，较弱 [WM13]；DNA 模型的变体改善了调控类任务，但其零样本的参考-变异相似度较弱（AUROC 为 0.503 至 0.574）[WM14]。

Vision papers propose a virtual cell with active learning and self-driving laboratories [WM24, WM25]. LabCompass is the first retrieved system that couples a generative cell-state model with action selection in a lab-in-the-loop setting; its readout is protein-level, the preprint is unreviewed, and document 09 gives the details [WM27, WM28, WM29, WM30].

> 愿景类论文提出带有主动学习和自驱动实验室的虚拟细胞 [WM24, WM25]。LabCompass 是检索到的第一个在实验室闭环情境中把生成式细胞状态模型与动作选择结合起来的系统；其读出是蛋白水平的，预印本未经评审，细节见文档 09 [WM27, WM28, WM29, WM30]。

Trajectory and intervention models supply transition cores. PerturbGen propagates an early perturbation to later states [WM33, MD59]; ARTEMIS couples a VAE with an unbalanced Schrödinger bridge, but its in-silico perturbations are post hoc state edits without experimental ground truth [WM34]; scDiffEq and PRESCIENT impose interventions as in-silico edits of states [WM36, WM37]; and DiRL uses a single-cell foundation model as the simulator for reinforcement learning, beating random perturbation baselines with a 69% win rate (abstract level, self-reported) [WM35].

> 轨迹和干预模型提供转移核心。PerturbGen 把早期扰动传播到后期状态 [WM33, MD59]；ARTEMIS 把 VAE 与非平衡薛定谔桥结合，但其计算机内扰动是事后的状态编辑，没有实验真值 [WM34]；scDiffEq 和 PRESCIENT 以计算机内的状态编辑来施加干预 [WM36, WM37]；DiRL 把单细胞基础模型用作强化学习的模拟器，以 69% 的胜率击败随机扰动基线（摘要层面，自报）[WM35]。

Planning styles differ. Gradient guidance steers a pretrained flow by an endpoint gradient [WM28] or by differentiating through the whole generation [WM39], with an optimal-control view [WM40]; gradient-free guidance suits non-differentiable objectives at the cost of many forward passes [WM46]; the choice depends on dimension and smoothness [WM45], per-step weights can be calibrated [WM44] and multi-objective targets need a front rather than one cap [WM47]. Sampling-based planning in a learned latent space needs no differentiable objective, as in V-JEPA 2-AC, which searches action sequences with the cross-entropy method and re-plans [WM38]. Steering a masked generative model by Metropolis–Hastings moves toward a target state but needs a same-perturbation anchor [WM42], an additive library baseline reached AUROC 0.91 on 63 reprogramming protocols [WM43], and dose-schedule theory shows that the form of induced tolerance flips the optimal plan [WM41].

> 规划风格各不相同。梯度引导通过终点梯度 [WM28] 或通过对整个生成过程求导 [WM39] 来推动预训练的流，并有最优控制的视角 [WM40]；无梯度引导适合不可微的目标，代价是大量前向传播 [WM46]；方法的选择取决于维度和平滑性 [WM45]，逐步权重可以校准 [WM44]，多目标需要的是一条前沿而不是单一上限 [WM47]。在学得的潜空间中做基于采样的规划不需要可微目标，如 V-JEPA 2-AC，它用交叉熵方法搜索动作序列并重新规划 [WM38]。用 Metropolis–Hastings 引导掩码生成模型能向目标状态移动，但需要同扰动的锚点 [WM42]；一个加性文库基线在 63 个重编程方案上达到 AUROC 0.91 [WM43]；剂量方案的理论表明，诱导耐受的形式会翻转最优方案 [WM41]。

## 3. A test battery for the "world model" claim / “世界模型”主张的检验清单

| Test | 检验 | What passes | 通过标准 | Data needed | 所需数据 | Source IDs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-step rollout | 多步展开 | Predictions at successive doses or times from one starting state stay stable and beat independent one-step predictions on held-out measurements | 从一个起始状态出发、在连续剂量或时间点上的预测保持稳定，并在留出的测量上优于独立的单步预测 | Dose series or time courses | 剂量系列或时间进程 | WM17 |
| State update | 状态更新 | A perturbation changes the latent state in a way that alters later predictions, not only the output label | 扰动改变潜在状态，从而改变后续预测，而不只是改变输出标签 | Sequential or combined perturbations | 序贯或组合扰动 | WM21 |
| Composition | 组合 | Held-out drug combinations are predicted from single-drug training and beat an additive baseline | 留出的药物组合由单药训练预测，并优于加性基线 | Combination data; none was found (Q67) | 组合数据；没有找到（Q67） | WM43, WM13 |
| Counterfactual variant swap | 反事实变异互换 | Infer the latent state once, swap the variant or action, and predict; the result matches the contrast seen between lineage-matched lines | 推断一次潜在状态，互换变异或动作后预测，结果与按谱系匹配的细胞系之间观察到的对比相符 | Lineage-matched mutant and non-mutant lines | 按谱系匹配的突变株与非突变株 | WM02, WM22 |
| Cross-modal consistency | 跨模态一致性 | Predictions agree across modalities | 不同模态的预测一致 | Multi-modal data; not available here | 多模态数据；此处不可得 | WM17 |
| Retrospective closed loop | 回溯式闭环 | A planner built on the model reaches conditions that meet a stated objective faster than random and greedy selection when conditions are revealed in rounds | 基于该模型的规划器，在分轮揭示条件时，比随机和贪心选择更快到达满足既定目标的条件 | A condition-resolved screen revealed in rounds | 分轮揭示的条件分辨筛选 | MD48, MD49, WM29 |
| Transport across mechanisms | 跨机制迁移 | Predictions do not transfer to a context whose mechanism differs unless the mechanism is modeled; checked on a simulator with adjustable mechanistic divergence | 除非对机制建模，预测不会迁移到机制不同的背景；用机制差异可调的模拟器检验 | Semi-synthetic data with known causal structure | 因果结构已知的半合成数据 | EV32 |

## 4. Judgments / 判断

J6 (confidence: medium). "World model" is currently closer to a narrative label than an established architecture class [WM21]. To justify it, a model must pass the tests in the table above: stable multi-step rollouts along dose or time, generalization to combinations of actions, and counterfactual genotype swaps. The data support dynamics weakly: no dataset with dense time courses crossed with drugs and genotypes was found (Q37, Q87), so multi-step tests may be limited to the dose axis. Two tests are added: a retrospective closed loop for decision quality [MD48, MD49] and a transportability check, because a variant that changes the mechanism should not be expected to transport without mechanism-aware conditioning [EV32].

> J6（置信度：中）。“世界模型”目前更接近一个叙事标签，而不是一个确立的架构类别 [WM21]。要证明这个名称合理，模型必须通过上表中的检验：沿剂量或时间的稳定多步展开、对动作组合的泛化，以及反事实的基因型互换。数据对动力学的支持较弱：没有找到把密集时间进程与药物和基因型交叉的数据集（Q37、Q87），所以多步检验可能只限于剂量轴。另外加入了两项检验：针对决策质量的回溯式闭环 [MD48, MD49]，以及可迁移性检查，因为改变机制的变异在没有感知机制的条件时不应指望能迁移 [EV32]。

J13 (confidence: medium). A latent-prediction model without a decoder still needs an explicit transcriptome readout to be evaluated at gene level, and the Cell-JEPA trade-off is a warning that such models may under-represent perturbation effect sizes [WM12, WM02].

> J13（置信度：中）。没有解码器的潜在预测模型仍需要显式的转录组读出才能在基因层面评估，Cell-JEPA 的取舍警示这类模型可能低估扰动的效应大小 [WM12, WM02]。

J25 (confidence: medium). LabCompass supports the decision half of a world model, not the dynamics half: its forward model gives end-state distributions given a protocol, with no multi-step rollout, state update or composition test in what was read [WM27, WM28]. A complete cell world model would pair a transition core [WM33, WM34, WM36, WM37, MD38] with a LabCompass-style planner (document 09, I8).

> J25（置信度：中）。LabCompass 支持世界模型的决策一半，而不支持动力学一半：其前向模型给出给定方案时的终态分布，所读内容中没有多步展开、状态更新或组合检验 [WM27, WM28]。完整的细胞世界模型应当把转移核心 [WM33, WM34, WM36, WM37, MD38] 与 LabCompass 式的规划器配对（文档 09，I8）。

J26 (confidence: low-medium). Gradient guidance and sampling-based search suit different objectives: sampling needs no differentiable objective [WM38, WM46], whereas gradient guidance can exploit regions where the surrogate is confidently wrong, so a regularizer and uncertainty-aware scoring have to limit it [WM28]. This is an inference from the descriptions, not a comparison made by any source.

> J26（置信度：低中）。梯度引导和基于采样的搜索适合不同的目标：采样不需要可微目标 [WM38, WM46]，而梯度引导可能利用代理模型自信出错的区域，所以必须用正则项和感知不确定性的打分加以限制 [WM28]。这是根据描述所做的推断，不是任何来源做出的比较。

## 5. Recommended actions / 行动建议

Adopt the operational definition and the test battery above before building anything, and record in advance which tests the available data can support [WM17, WM21].

> 在构建任何东西之前，采用上面的操作性定义和检验清单，并事先记录现有数据能支持哪些检验 [WM17, WM21]。

Treat self-labelled world models as baselines to be re-evaluated, not as evidence that the architecture works, because all are unverified preprints or repositories [WM06, WM10, WM11, WM20].

> 把自称世界模型的模型当作有待重新评估的基线，而不是该架构行之有效的证据，因为它们都是未经核验的预印本或仓库 [WM06, WM10, WM11, WM20]。

If a JEPA-style objective is used, add an explicit transcriptome readout and track effect-size accuracy next to state fidelity [WM12].

> 如果使用 JEPA 式目标，要加入显式的转录组读出，并在状态保真度之外跟踪效应大小的准确度 [WM12]。

Keep the world-model question separate from the performance question: a model can predict well without being a world model, and a world-model design can predict worse than a linear baseline.

> 把世界模型问题与性能问题分开：模型可以预测得很好却不是世界模型，世界模型设计也可能预测得比线性基线更差。

Treat LabCompass as the reference for the decision half, pair it with a transition core, and add the retrospective closed-loop test and the transportability simulator to the battery [WM27, WM28, MD48, EV32].

> 把 LabCompass 作为决策这一半的参考，将其与转移核心配对，并把回溯式闭环检验和可迁移性模拟器加入检验清单 [WM27, WM28, MD48, EV32]。

## 7. Evidence gaps / 证据缺口

No self-labelled world model was verified, and no source showed a world-model architecture beating a conditional generative model on any benchmark (Q02, Q41, Q90). The primary papers of Dreamer's successors were not retrieved beyond surveys (Q09, Q36). No source shows a decision-oriented world model beating a greedy baseline on transcriptomes (Q56, Q90, Q97), and the LabCompass full text was not read [WM27].

> 没有任何自称世界模型的模型得到核验，也没有来源显示世界模型架构在任何基准上优于条件生成模型（Q02、Q41、Q90）。Dreamer 后继者的原始论文只通过综述见到（Q09、Q36）。没有来源显示面向决策的世界模型在转录组上优于贪心基线（Q56、Q90、Q97），LabCompass 全文也没有读到 [WM27]。
